# SCENE PROMPT: "FIRST LIGHT"
### A 4:16 procedural film. One photon, from the core of the Sun to the back of your eye.

> Build `first_light_engine_prerequisites.md` first. This prompt assumes those modules exist. If one was cut or reduced, `docs/first_light_readiness.md` says which act it affects. Adjust that act honestly; do not fake around it.

Everything you see and hear is generated by code. The only imported asset is the music track (`app/public/audio/first_light.mp3`). No models, textures, HDRIs or sample files.

**The bar:** a viewer who has seen every title sequence and space documentary leans forward at 0:20 and doesn't breathe properly until 4:16. If any frame reads as a screensaver, a stock "cosmic" template or an explainer video, it has failed. Every requirement below exists because it shows up on screen or in the ear. If something you're building wouldn't be noticed by a viewer, don't spend time on it. If something would make a shot unforgettable, do it even if this brief doesn't mention it.

## 0. Before you build

1. Load the skills: `fx-engine`, `fx-scenes`, `fx-transitions`, `fx-procedural-modeling`, `fx-kinetic-typography`, `design-around-audio`, `sound-design-and-foley`, `visual-metaphors-and-abstraction`, `storyboarding-and-blocking`, `viopls-art-direction`, `video-assembly-and-finishing`, `fx-diagnostics`.
2. Analyse the song (`scripts/analyze_song.py`, using its `--verify` mode). Compare the detected downbeats, sections and silences against the expected structure in §3. Check especially that the hard stop after climax 1 and the start of the piano break are where you think they are. If anything looks off, show the user the timings and ask before building on them. The bar map in §3 is the intent; **the real song is the law.** Snap act boundaries and hero hits to real downbeats and onsets.
3. Write `app/src/scenes/first_light/visual_plan.md`: for each act, the metaphor, the hero frame described in one sentence, the lens and camera move, and the colour state. Finish the per-act cameras and photon paths in `photon.ts` at the same time (prerequisite P1b).
4. Build one act at a time. Render it, look at the frames, and only move on when it clears the bar.

**How the pieces fit:** every scene is a pure function of `t`, because transitions can only reach scenes through `api.sampleScene(id, t)`. The photon's position and the cameras live in `photon.ts`, and every scene and transition reads from there, which is what keeps the photon steady through every seam. Light colours come from `spectrum.ts`.

## 1. Director's intent

A photon made in the Sun's core spends about **170,000 years** bouncing through plasma so dense it moves millimetres between collisions. When it escapes, it crosses 150 million km in **8 minutes 20 seconds**. It falls through a sky, splits inside a raindrop, and ends in **less than a billionth of a second**, absorbed by one molecule in the back of an eye. Its whole existence ends in the act of being seen.

No narration. Emotional arc: **Pressure → Struggle → Release → Silence → Wonder → Ecstasy → Intimacy → one quiet sting.** The sting: the last thing the photon does is become *you* seeing it.

**Honest physics, stylised.** Photons don't bend in magnetic fields, so in the aurora act everything else curves and the photon doesn't. Aurora is real oxygen emission (557.7 nm green, 630.0 nm red). The sky is blue because of Rayleigh scattering. The rainbow sits at about 42°. Accuracy is a visual weapon here: true things look stranger and better than invented ones.

**The one licence, taken knowingly.** Strictly, light from the core is absorbed and re-emitted countless times on the way out, and a single photon has a single wavelength. The film treats the light's whole lineage as one character: a packet of sunlight that reads as white until the droplet, where it reveals the colours it was carrying. From then on, we follow the red one home. Everything else stays honest.

## 2. The rules that carry the film

### 2.1 Colour is physics
Light in the frame is never decorative. It is the physically correct colour of the light at that moment. Surfaces may be neutral; light may not.

| Act | Colour state | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Core | Overexposed violet-white, pushed into clipping | Gamma/X-ray energies, beyond visible |
| Convection | Blackbody climb, ember (~3000 K) to white-gold (~5800 K) | Losing energy as it rises |
| Breach | Blinding white-gold | The photosphere |
| Space | One white point on true black | Nothing to scatter it |
| Aurora | 557.7 nm green + 630.0 nm red only | Oxygen emission |
| Sky | Black deepening to blue with optical depth | Rayleigh scattering |
| Droplet | The full spectrum, the only time every hue appears | Dispersion |
| Eye | Warm biology: iris stroma, retinal red-orange | The first non-light colours |
| Coda | One white point | Back where it began |

Because each act owns its palette, the Droplet's full spectrum lands as a release the viewer has been starved of for three minutes. Protect that: no rainbow hues anywhere before Act 7.

### 2.2 The photon is the anchor
A tiny, painfully bright point with a physically motivated glow (airy disk, subtle diffraction spikes), not a radial gradient. It's in almost every frame and carries the eye across every seam: **the cut happens around it, never through it.**

### 2.3 Screen geography
- The photon travels broadly left to right across the film and lands dead centre in the Eye.
- Camera roll stays under 4° except in the Breach.
- Rising means escaping; falling (the Sky) means arriving.

### 2.4 Restraint
- Five text events in the whole film (§5). No labels, arrows or explanations.
- Camera shake only in the Core and the Breach, motivated by pressure.
- No lens flares beyond the photon's own optics. No dust motes, no starfield wallpaper. Stars only in the Crossing, sparse, no twinkle in vacuum.
- No glitches, RGB-split, hologram UI, hex grids or plexus networks. The neurons are real dendritic trees.
- **Silence and emptiness are effects.** The Void Cut and the Crossing are worth more than anything you could fill them with.

## 3. Structure (90 BPM, 96 bars ≈ 4:16; re-anchor to the real song)

| # | Act | Bars | Nominal | Music |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Cold Open | 0–6 | 0:00–0:16 | Sub drone, one high sine |
| 1 | The Core | 6–22 | 0:16–0:59 | Pulsing low strings, irregular percussion |
| 2 | Convection | 22–32 | 0:59–1:25 | Rising arpeggio, build |
| 3 | The Breach | 32–38 | 1:25–1:41 | Climax 1, then a hard stop |
| 4 | The Crossing | 38–54 | 1:41–2:24 | Near silence, lone felt piano |
| 5 | Magnetosphere | 54–64 | 2:24–2:51 | Pulse returns, choir, build |
| 6 | The Sky | 64–72 | 2:51–3:12 | Swelling strings, drums return |
| 7 | The Droplet | 72–80 | 3:12–3:33 | **Main climax** |
| 8 | The Eye | 80–90 | 3:33–4:00 | Sudden intimacy, piano, heartbeat pulse |
| 9 | Coda | 90–96 | 4:00–4:16 | One sustained chord, decay to silence |

### Act 0: Cold Open
Two seconds of true black and sub drone. One pixel ignites left of centre, so small the viewer isn't sure it's there. Over 12 s it gains its airy disk and spikes and breathes with the drone's beating. On the last downbeat, the black around it shifts. It was never empty (T1).

### Act 1: The Core
**A crowd you can't get through.** Extreme macro, locked to the photon. Hundreds of thousands of pale, overexposed plasma particles press in from all sides, lit only by the photon. The photon follows `walkPath(SEED)`: a dash, a collision flash, a new direction, several collisions per beat, with the hardest ones on the kicks. Each leaves a fading hairline trace. The camera trails on a critically damped spring and never cuts. Depth of field so shallow only the photon is sharp; heavy grain. It should feel suffocating.

Around bar 14, a slow pull-back (dolly and FOV together, so it doesn't read as a zoom) reveals the trail: thousands of zig-zags forming a dense, beautiful ball of light-thread. **Hero frame: the Ball of Yarn.** Text 1 arrives here.

### Act 2: Convection
**Being carried.** The outermost thread of the tangle straightens into a streamline and the camera matches its tangent: same curve, new meaning (an in-camera morph, not a cut). The photon is now part of a flow. Vast Rayleigh–Bénard convection rolls of raymarched, emissive plasma climb around it, colour rising along the blackbody ramp with altitude and accelerating with the arpeggio. **Hero frame:** a top-down view of solar granulation (thousands of convecting cells, bright centres, dark lanes) that tilts to horizontal as the photon rises through one granule toward its bright skin.

### Act 3: The Breach
**Breaking the surface of water from below.** The granule skin tents up under the photon and ruptures (T3). The one licensed roll: a slow 25° roll as the camera bursts above the surface. A prominence arches overhead, thousands of plasma ribbons on real magnetic-loop geometry, with coronal rain sliding down them. Bloom near clipping, god rays off the limb. The last bar crescendos, and on the downbeat **everything stops** (T4).

### Act 4: The Crossing
**The loneliest commute in the universe.** Half a second of true black and true silence, then the photon fades in at the same screen position. Now it's the only thing alive in infinite black. Locked-off wide camera; the Sun recedes behind it. The photon moves at light speed and seems to stand still, because space is that big. Sell scale with very little: a planet crescent passing with correct phase lighting, then a slow orbit move revealing Earth as a blue point that grows to a disk. Text 2 here. The felt piano carries it. **Do not fill this act.** If in doubt, remove something.

### Act 5: Magnetosphere
**Everything else gets caught.** Solar-wind particles arrive alongside the photon in a different register (dim, granular, ash-toned). Earth's field lines draw on in world space as the photon enters the bow shock: the black was full of structure all along (rhymes with T1). The particles curl along the field lines and spiral toward the poles. **The photon goes perfectly straight.** **Hero frame:** hundreds of curving paths and one ruler-straight line of light through them. Text 3 rides that line. At the pole the particles hit the atmosphere and the aurora ignites: 557.7 nm curtains with 630.0 nm upper fringes, vertical ray structure and slow fold waves. The photon passes straight through.

### Act 6: The Sky
**Arriving home.** No cut from Act 5: the frame's grade deepens from black through indigo to blue, driven by optical depth. In one shot, short-wavelength sibling photons ricochet off sideways while red passes straight, so the sky turns blue *because of what we just saw*. The photon falls through raymarched cumulus with a silver edge into rain: instanced, motion-blurred streaks. One droplet is lit from inside. The photon is falling toward it.

### Act 7: The Droplet (hero act)
**The photon shows what it's made of.** As the camera approaches the drop, the world appears inverted inside it, then the camera crosses the surface with a total-internal-reflection rim (T7). Inside, the white light opens into a fan of the spectrum: refract in, reflect once, refract out, red at about 42° and violet at about 40°, glowing rays inside a glass sphere with caustics on its inner wall. Text 4 is etched there. On the main climax downbeat, pull back hard: thousands of droplets do the same thing at once and a full primary rainbow assembles across the frame, with a faint reversed secondary bow and Alexander's dark band between them. **The most saturated, densest moment in the film.** Then the camera rides one red ray out of the bow, toward an eye.

### Act 8: The Eye
**The end of the line is a beginning.** The drop becomes the eyeball: its rim becomes the limbus and the rainbow bands become the iris fibres (T8). Procedural iris stroma (radial fibres, crypts, collarette). The pupil constricts to the light on the heartbeat hit. Push through the pupil and the dark vitreous onto the retina, a field of rods and cones. The red photon strikes one red-sensitive (L) cone, not a rod; rods barely respond to red. One retinal molecule snaps from bent to straight with a tiny satisfying click. **The photon disappears.** In its place, a signal flickers down a dendrite and neurons fire in a branching cascade, pulses synced to the piano.

### Act 9: Coda
Pull back from the cascade. It morphs, vertex by vertex, into the Act 1 Ball of Yarn: the same `walkPath(SEED)` data (T9). The start and end of the photon's life are the same shape. Hold. The tangle collapses to one white point at the exact screen position of the first pixel. Text 5. The chord decays. True black, then 2 s of silence.

## 4. Transitions

Six bespoke transitions in `app/src/transitions/`. Study the existing ones for technique; reuse none as-is. Each is a physical event in the photon's life, and the photon stays put on screen through all of them.

| ID | Seam | What happens | What the viewer feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Cold Open → Core | The black resolves into particles through exposure and depth-of-field change. No cut. | "Empty" was full. |
| T3 | Convection → Breach | The granule skin tents under the photon, tears radially, and the camera follows through. On a hit. | Breaking the surface. |
| T4 | Breach → Crossing | **The Void Cut.** One frame to exact black and exact silence on the downbeat, a 0.5 s hold, then the photon fades in at the same spot. | The biggest contrast in the film. A gut punch. |
| T7 | Sky → Droplet | A real refractive lens: the world appears inverted through the drop, the surface fills frame, the camera crosses with a TIR rim. | A shot people pause on. |
| T8 | Droplet → Eye | Sphere match-cut: drop to eyeball, rim to limbus, rainbow bands to iris fibres, on one downbeat. | Shape, colour and position rhyme. |
| T9 | Eye → Coda | Dendrites morph into the Act 1 tangle by vertex correspondence, not a crossfade. | Recognition, then chills. |

The other seams (Core → Convection, Crossing → Magnetosphere, Magnetosphere → Sky) happen inside the shots as described in each act. They should feel like one continuous camera, not transitions.

## 5. Typography: five events, all inside the world

Text sits in the world: lit by the scene, in the depth of field, occluding and occluded. Engine grotesk or mono, set with discipline.

| # | Act | Text | Treatment |

| 1 | Core | `170,000 YEARS` | Rolls up from 0 during the pull-back, half-buried in particles |
| 2 | Crossing | `8 MIN 20 S` | Single-stroke vector font, plotted stroke by stroke with the piano, ash white |
| 3 | Magnetosphere | `IT DOES NOT BEND` | Extruded 3D along the photon's straight path; the particle paths curve around the letters |

## 6. Sound

Every effect is synthesised (seeded, no sample files) and bound to the visual event it belongs to, so it follows the picture. Music ducks 3–6 dB under hero cues. Effects pan with screen position and stay out of the music's key range. Master around −14 LUFS.

| Act | Cue | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Ignition | A 4 kHz sine rising out of nothing, a sub at 30 Hz beating against 31 Hz |
| 1 | Collisions | Sharp granular ticks, seeded pitch, panned with the photon, louder on kick-locked hits |
| 1 | Pressure | Filtered brown noise; the filter opens with the pull-back |
| 2 | Convection | Slow band-passed roar with a rising Shepard layer |
| 3 | Rupture | A taut low tone bending up, then a wet tearing crackle |
| 3 | Prominence | A deep thrum that follows the loop across the stereo field |
| 3→4 | **The Void Cut** | Exact digital silence for 0.5 s. The most important sound in the film. |
| 4 | Planet / Earth | Barely-there low "air" swells far under the piano. Space is silent. |
| 5 | Field lines | Crystalline FM shimmer tracking each line as it draws |
| 5 | Aurora | Very quiet, broad, slowly beating harmonic pads |
| 6 | Atmosphere / rain | Rising filtered wind with high fizz for the scattering shot; granular rain whose density follows the droplets |
| 7 | Entry | A glassy ping with a reverse-reverb lead-in landing on the surface crossing |
| 7 | Split | Seven sine voices fanning in pitch with the rays |
| 7 | Rainbow | A full-spectrum shimmer swell into the climax downbeat |
| 8 | Pupil | Soft, muffled organic tightening |
| 8 | Molecule click | Tiny, dry, mono, centred. The quietest cue with the most presence. |
| 8 | Neurons | Soft electrical pops in key, on the piano onsets |
| 9 | Collapse | Every layer converges to one sine and fades with the light |

**Hero hits** that must land on the frame of the real onset: T3, T4, the Breach roll, the Earth reveal, the straight photon in Act 5, the rainbow downbeat, T8, the pupil, the molecule click, the final word.

## 7. Craft

- **Output:** 1920×1080, 60 fps. Measure in 1080p units.
- **Motion:** every camera move has a lens, a motivation and a named easing curve. Nothing linear unless it's physically linear; the photon in vacuum *is* linear, and that's the point.
- **Scrub-safe:** seeded randomness only (`mulberry32`), no wall-clock time. Any frame renders the same in any order. Transitions depend on it.
- **Preview at 30+ fps** at 1080p where you can; the offline render can be heavier.
- **Missing capability?** Build the smallest correct version in the engine under the `fx-engine-dev` rules, note it in `docs/first_light_readiness.md`, and carry on. Don't hack it into a scene file.

## 9. Never

- Space clichés: flare streaks, nebula wallpaper, twinkling stars, whooshes in vacuum, a spinning Earth with a blue glow.
- Plexus networks, hex grids, holo UI, HUDs, data streams, glitches.
- Explanatory text, labels or arrows.
- A colour of light that §2.1 doesn't justify.
- Cuts that hide the photon, or transitions that look like presets.
- Filling silence.
