# cymatics — storyboard

A black metal plate scattered with sand, vibrating. As the driving frequency climbs, the sand migrates — chaos snapping into order — forming the crisp geometric nodal lines of each Chladni mode: first a simple cross, then diagonals, then intricate lattice stars. Sound made visible; pitch as geometry. 30s, single shot.


## Beats
- 0–3s: sand scattered random across the plate, a low hum beginning — grains jitter.
- 3–9s: first mode — grains migrate to a simple cross pattern; order emerging.
- 9–17s: frequency steps — the cross dissolves, grains dance, a diagonal lattice forms.
- 17–26s: higher modes — increasingly intricate star/lattice figures, transitions faster.
- 26–30s: the highest figure holds — fine filigree lines of sand in rim light.

## Camera specification
- Lens: normal; aperture 5 — shallow DoF softness at plate edges.
- Position vector: slow overhead drift, slightly oblique — reading the pattern while keeping materiality.
- Focal point: plate center.
- Movement language: patient scientific observation — the pattern is everything.
