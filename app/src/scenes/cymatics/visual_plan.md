# cymatics — visual plan

Acoustics register: sound made visible. Chladni figures are the rare phenomenon where an invisible quantity (frequency) becomes exact geometry — pitch you can see. The catalog has no acoustics scene and no scene where mathematics manifests as physical granular behavior.

Don't forget to ground yourself when its time USE THE SKILL and REREAD IT ALL

## mapping table
| metaphor | materials | changes over time |
|---|---|---|
| sound's hidden geometry | sand grains on a black steel plate | grains migrate from chaos into crisp nodal-line figures |
| frequency as form | successive Chladni modes — cross, diagonals, lattice stars | patterns dissolve and reform at each frequency step, growing in complexity |
| order from chaos | random initial scatter | each transition: turbulence then snap-to-order |
| precision | sharp thin nodal lines vs empty antinode fields | lines sharpen as each mode settles |

## anti-cliché audit
- NOT a music-visualizer aesthetic — no waveforms, no pulsing colors, no neon
- no mystical "sound frequency healing" framing — this is physics demonstration, materially honest
- no camera excitement — the phenomenon carries everything

## focal hierarchy
1. the sand figure itself — nodal line geometry
2. grain motion during transitions — the dance
3. plate materiality — blackened steel, rim light
4. void darkness beyond plate edges
