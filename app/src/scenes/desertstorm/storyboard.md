# desertstorm — storyboard

20s single take. Backdrop shader at PZ=−80 (PW 320, PH 140), camera fov 40°.

| t | beat | what's on screen |
|---|------|------------------|
| 0–4 | still road | Empty highway to vanishing point; poles left, fence right, mesa rim, amber sun glow right; shimmer pools; caption "route 17 — 6:40 pm" |
| 4–8 | wall rises | Ochre wall enters left, ragged top rolling; left sky browns; wires vibrate, bag whips, first streaks; crow fighting in from the right; caption "haboob front on the horizon" |
| 8–12 | front arrives | Edge sweeps across: pole 6 → pole 1 swallowed in order; god-ray slits; truck headlights flare then cut; camera shakes; caption "the wall arrives" → "zero visibility" |
| 11–14 | swallowed | Featureless stratified dust; grain streaks at full lick; only texture survives |
| 14–19 | thinning | Trailing haze fades; road/poles re-emerge in dim amber; crow perched on the fence post; caption "the tail drags east" |

## Motion notes
- Wall advance is left→right over ~7s (ss 4.5→11.5); sequential swallow is
  emergent from the coverage mask, no per-element timing.
- Tumbleweeds: 4 seeds, each approaches z 11→3.2 on fract phases, bouncing
  (|sin| hops ∝ 1/z), drifting toward the shoulder; shadow ellipse on tarmac.
- Truck: headlights only — two glows at z 26→7, flicker; die at t≈11–12 as
  the front crosses road center.
- Crow: path mix(fly, perch, ss(9,10)); flap rate eases on landing; perched
  for the rest of the take.
