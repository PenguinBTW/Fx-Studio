# cavernecho — storyboard

24 s · 16:9 · 60 fps · three 8-second acts, continuous CameraRig creep
toward the cave-mouth shaft.

| Act | Window | Beat | Camera | Captions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–8 | hush + drips | slow creep toward the shaft | FIG.01 HUSH AND DRIP |
| 2 | 8–16 | bat pass → multi-drop chorus | holds on the shaft–pool axis | FIG.02 WINGS IN THE SHAFT |
| 3 | 16–24 | deepest quiet + salamander | settles on the pool edge | FIG.03 QUIET WATER |

## Shot logic

- **t≈3:** shaft rakes down upper-right, stalactites rim-lit, first
  droplets falling from seeded tips, rings spreading on the pool.
- **t≈11:** a bat silhouette crosses the shaft once — wing-beat envelope
  visible as it passes the bright band.
- **t≈13–15:** multi-drop chorus — the drip cadence peaks, rings
  cross-cut each other on the pool.
- **t≈20:** the calm beat — drips thin to near nothing; a salamander
  shade scuttles along the floor band.

## Focal points

1. Shaft → pool axis — light column down onto the water.
2. Ring field — expanding ellipses where each drip lands.
3. The bat pass — the one moving silhouette in the light.
