# vaulted — storyboard (20 s, 4 acts)

| t (s) | act | caption | action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | open | `FIG 01 — A ROOF OPEN TO HEAVEN` | Low dolly begins down the nave. Moon shafts hang pale and wide; the ruin is mostly silhouette with flagstone sheen. Candle already burning in the sconce — small warm point in the cold frame. |
| 5–10 | light | `FIG 02 — LIGHT THROUGH THE BREACH` | Shafts strengthen to full intensity; dust motes stream down inside them. Shaft direction sways a degree as if cloud thins at the gap. Broken column tops catch the light edge. |
| 10–15 | flame | `FIG 03 — ONE SMALL FLAME` | Camera closes toward the sconce column; the warm/cold contrast peaks — amber capital against blue nave. Candle flicker strongest here; ivy leaves catch its edge. |
| 15–20 | settle | `FIG 04 — THE STONES KEEP WATCH` | Shafts ease back to opening intensity; camera drift slows. Wide feel again — the ruin resumes its vigil, the flame still burning. |

Camera: single continuous dolly, no cuts — the "acts" are intensity
arcs on light and flicker, not edits.
