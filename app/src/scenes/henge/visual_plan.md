# henge — visual plan

Archaeoastronomy register: timekeeping as architecture. The stone circle is humanity's first clock — light and shadow sweeping a calibrated instrument. The catalog has no ancient structure and no scene where shadow itself is the moving subject. henge's motion is almost entirely cast shadow.

Don't forget to ground yourself when its time USE THE SKILL and REREAD IT ALL

## mapping table
| abstract concept | physical visual |
|---|---|
| deep time / ancient timekeeping | stones weathered, lichened, monumental |
| the day's arc | shadows sweeping the ground like clock hands |
| cosmic order | the precise final alignment — the instrument still works |
| patience | near-stillness; only light moves fast |

## anti-cliché audit
- NOT a fantasy mystical ruin — no fog machines, no magic glow; the stones are geology and the light is astronomy
- no lens flare at alignment — the spear of light through the gap is geometry, not a filter
- no tourists, no ropes, no modern world

## focal hierarchy
1. the trilithon gap — the instrument's sightline, always the target
2. the sweeping shadows — the motion itself
3. stone texture — flanks raking in low light
4. mist and grass — atmosphere

## light
One directional sun, warm-cold shifting with elevation (blackbody-ish). Sky gradient dome: indigo dawn → pale noon → amber dusk. Sun elevation drives both shadow direction and sky color — one clock.
