# henge — storyboard

A stone circle on a dark plain, a full day compressed to thirty seconds. The sun arcs low across the sky and the stones' long shadows sweep the ground like clock hands — until the final moment, when the sun drops exactly into the trilithon gap and light spears through the circle's heart. 30s, single continuous shot.

## Beats
- 0–5s: pre-dawn — the stones as silhouettes, sky deep indigo, mist low on the plain.
- 5–12s: sunrise — light ignites the eastern sky, shadows fire westward, long and sharp.
- 12–22s: the sweep — the sun arcs, shadows rotate across the grass like a sundial's hand, stone flanks raking gold then gray.
- 22–28s: descent — the light warms again, shadows stretching east now.
- 28–30s: the alignment — the sun drops into the trilithon gap; a blade of light spears the circle center; fade.

## Camera specification
- Lens: wide-normal; aperture engine-scale 4 — everything deep.
- Position vector: slow orbit outside the circle, radius ~14, low pitch — the stones monumental against the sky; holds through the alignment.
- Focal point: the trilithon gap throughout — the scene's narrative target.
- Movement language: patient drift, no handheld — this is geological time, not documentary.
