## 2. Anti-Cliché Audit (Forbidden Tropes & Replacements)

- **Forbidden Cliché:** Floating generic network webs (floating glowing spheres connected by bezier wires in empty void).  
  **Grounding Replacement:** Continuous grounded copper bus rails and subterranean structural conduit trays mounted to monolithic concrete/basalt walls with visible mechanical expansion brackets.
- **Forbidden Cliché:** Corporate Memphis / Pastel claymorphism or floating translucent holograms.  
  **Grounding Replacement:** Cold, heavy, tactile PBR materials—machined titanium, raw cast basalt, dark industrial polymer, and incandescent quartz filaments with physically motivated directional shadows.
- **Forbidden Cliché:** Meaningless HUD clutter, decorative radial grids, or random percentage tickers.  
  **Grounding Replacement:** Diegetic architectural typography physically stamped into the machine chassis or etched into metal plates with dark knockout understroke halos (`haloWidth: 6`), obeying physical depth and camera occlusion.
- **Forbidden Cliché:** Subjects floating in ungrounded infinite grey void.  
  **Grounding Replacement:** Massive structural ground plane with brushed metal plates, tactile floor seams, micro-scratches, contact shadows, and subtle volumetric atmospheric haze motes.

## 3. Spatial Hierarchy & Eye-Tracking Map

### Shot 1 & 2: The Core Crucible

- **Ambient Floor (10% Visual Weight):** Milled floor plates, cryogenic vapor motes, and subtle specular floor reflections.

- **Primary Hero (60% Visual Weight):** Foreground titanium data carrier capsule on precision copper rails ($960, 540$).
- **Secondary Support (30% Visual Weight):** Cavernous architectural canyon walls stretching along $+Z$, repeating transit pylons, and diegetic stamped bulkhead plate (`180ns LATENCY BOUNDARY`).
- **Ambient Floor (10% Visual Weight):** Deep void substrate trench reflections and raking 6500K rim-light highlights.

## 5. Lighting & Physical Optics Strategy

- **Key Light (Act 1):** Motivated 3200K warm tungsten Planckian blackbody radiator located inside the core crucible ($[2.5, 4.0, 3.0]$), casting sharp chiaroscuro directional shadows across the floor.
- **Rim Light (Act 2):** High-contrast 6500K cool daylight rim luminaire ($[-4.0, 6.0, -8.0]$) raking across the top edges of the copper rails and titanium capsules.
- **Physical Optics:** Thin-lens Circle-of-Confusion Depth of Field (`dof.ts`) with a 9-blade cinema iris diaphragm (`blades: 9`), aperture $22.0\text{px}$, transitioning from deep focus to macro telephoto bokeh during the final inspection.
- **Cinematic Optical Post:** 7-mip HalfFloat bloom (`bloom: 0.35`, `bloomThreshold: 0.85`), subtle edge halation (`halation: 0.18`), micro film grain (`grain: 0.05`), and edge chromatic aberration (`ca: 2.0px`).
