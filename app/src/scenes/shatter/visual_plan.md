# shatter — visual plan

Destruction register: fracture as revelation. A breaking plate is the universal slow-motion subject because fracture is normally invisible — faster than perception. Compressed time makes the crack propagation itself the star. The catalog has formation/growth registers (verdant, geode) but nothing of breaking — creation's mirror.

Don't forget to ground yourself when its time USE THE SKILL and REREAD IT ALL

## mapping table
| metaphor | materials | changes over time |
|---|---|---|
| fragility | white porcelain glaze — hard, thin, precious | whole → fractured → dispersed |
| hidden structure | voronoi shard boundaries — the plate's internal stress map made visible | invisible while whole, revealed at the strike |
| irreversibility | shards never reassemble — drift monotonically apart | the halo expands through the entire runtime |
| frozen violence | extreme slow motion | crack race in <1s, then ballistic drift in cinematic slow-mo |

## anti-cliché audit
- NOT a dramatic explosion — no dust burst, no shockwave ring, no camera shake
- no spinning-matrix bullet-time camera — the camera is nearly still; the material performs
- no colored lighting games — one rim light, one key, white porcelain doing the work

## focal hierarchy
1. the crack race — the one-second star of the scene
2. shard drift and tumble — the bloom
3. glaze specular flashes as shards rotate through the key light
4. the void — negative space
