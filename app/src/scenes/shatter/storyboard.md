# shatter — storyboard

A porcelain plate hanging in a void, struck. In extreme slow motion the fracture race across the glaze — cracks spider from the impact point at superspeed, then the shards begin their drift apart, rotating lazily, glaze catching the light as the plate's ghost of a circle expands into a halo of fragments. 30s, single shot.

## Beats
- 0–3s: the plate whole, serene, rim-lit — the calm that makes the breaking matter.
- 3–4s: the strike — a flash-crack instant; fracture lines race across the glaze.
- 4–15s: the separation — shards drift apart along ballistic paths, rotating slowly, the circle becoming a halo.
- 15–28s: the bloom — fragments spread wide, tumbling, each catching light on its glazed face as it turns.
- 28–30s: hold — the expanded constellation of shards, motion slowing into stillness.

## Camera specification
- Lens: normal-wide; aperture engine-scale 5 — slight DoF softness on far shards.
- Position vector: slow push-in during the calm, holding through the shatter, slight pull-back as the halo expands.
- Focal point: plate center throughout.
- Movement language: patient, almost clinical — the drama is in the material, not the camera.
