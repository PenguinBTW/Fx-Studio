# harmonograph — storyboard

| t (s) | beat |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Void. A single point of light ignites and begins to move; the first ribbon writes behind it. |
| 3–10 | Second pen joins in rose; the two curves weave, never touching, harmonic interference visible. |
| 10–18 | Third pen in violet; the full braid forms. Camera orbit slowly reveals the 3D structure — flat-seeming lines gain depth. |
| 18–26 | Amplitudes decaying: the pens spiral inward, lines tightening toward the center knot. |
| 26–30 | The pens come nearly to rest, glowing nodes at the heart of the finished drawing; the braid hangs complete, slowly rotating in frame. |
