# harmonograph — visual plan

## Concept
A harmonograph drawing itself in light: three luminous pendulum tips trace 3D
Lissajous ribbons through a dark void, each line fading in as it is written,
spectral color shifting along its arc length. Pure mathematical beauty — no
environment, no props, no text. The motion IS the subject.

## Register gap
Catalog: naturalistic exteriors, macro interior (fernhollow), architectural
(atrium). Missing: abstract/mathematical. This is the canonical form of it —
closed-form curves are literally what the engine is built on, so the scene is
the engine demonstrating its own nature.

## Style guidance applied
- forge lesson: abstract must not become sci-fi UI — no grids, no HUD, no wireframe
  chrome. Just light on black, like long-exposure photography of a real harmonograph.
- Terrest lesson: camera slow and motivated — one gentle orbit, letting the drawing
  write itself.

## Palette
- Void black background.
- Ribbon hues by arc position: amber → rose → violet spectral drift, HDR-bright
  cores for bloom.
- Pen tips: hot white-gold points.

## Movement
- Three ribbons write over ~26s at different frequency ratios (3:2, 5:4, 4:3 +
  phase offsets), amplitudes decaying exactly as a real damped pendulum.
- Camera: slow orbit, radius constant, slight vertical drift; the drawing rotates
  in frame like a sculpture.
