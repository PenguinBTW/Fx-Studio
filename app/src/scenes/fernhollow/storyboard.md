# fernhollow — storyboard

| t (s) | beat |
|---|---|
| 0–4 | Open in near-dark shade. Spores drift through the first light shaft; focus deep. |
| 4–10 | Camera creeps forward at snail height; focus racks to the snail on the log as its eyestalks emerge into a shaft. |
| 10–18 | The glide: snail crosses the frame left-to-right along the mossy log; fronds sway overhead; spore density rises in the main shaft. |
| 18–24 | Focus racks past the snail to the spore field — snail dissolves into bokeh; motes bloom into discs. |
| 24–30 | Rack back to the snail at the log's end; it pauses into the last shaft as the light warms; settle. |
