# lighthouse — visual plan

A beam sweeping a foggy rocky headland at night, ~20 s, fully procedural f(t).

## World (single backdrop pass + squall layer)
- Sky: moon low right with halo; hashed stars gated by fbm cloud breaks; drifting mist banks.
- Headland: hashed granite silhouette left-of-center; lighthouse tower + warm lamp room.
- Beam: soft-edged wedge rotating 360° on closed-form θ(t); intensity fades through mist banks, flares clean; a synchronized light patch slides across cliff and swell lines.
- Sea: swell bands on sine fields; white-cap glints where the beam patch passes; vessel light crosses far right once.
- Squall: transparent streak layer, gated to its own envelope, faintly refracting the beam.

## Camera
CameraRig slow push z +24 → +10 toward the headland, mild pitch hold, faint roll.

## Beats
1. t 0–6: establish — moon, mist, tower lit.
2. t 6–12: first full sweep — beam arcs the frame, patch runs the swell.
3. t 12–16: squall passage — streaks cross, beam refracts/dims.
4. t 16–20: clear-star reveal — mist thins, stars out, vessel light gone.
