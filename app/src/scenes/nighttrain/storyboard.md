# nighttrain — storyboard

Entry `nighttrain/night_viaduct`, t = 0–20.

## Shots

1. **0–2.5 — The valley holds its breath.** Establishing: moonlit sky,
   halo'd moon high right, star field, drifting cloud wisps. The stone
   viaduct spans the frame — deck band, arched openings, cold rim light —
   over a valley floor where the river threads a silver moon lane.
   Camera nearly still, slow drift.
   Caption: *the valley holds its breath*

2. **4–6 — A light comes down the line.** Before any silhouette shows, a
   warm glow swells at the left edge — the headlight arriving around the
   curve — and a faint streak warms the river under the first arch.
   Caption: *a light comes down the line*

3. **6–15 — The crossing.** The engine enters left: silhouette with
   stack, cab, and a soft piston-bob; the headlight cone sweeps gently
   with the track; smoke puffs seed above the stack, rising and trailing
   back. Three carriages follow with flickering warm windows. The block
   signal snaps green → red as the front passes it; the watchman's hut
   holds one lit window. Mid-crossing, birds startle from beneath an
   arch and scatter upward.

4. **15–17.5 — Tail lamp.** The last carriage clears; its red lamp rides
   right and shrinks into the dark while the headlight glow dies off the
   river.

5. **17–20 — Only smoke remembers.** The viaduct is empty again; the
   smoke trail keeps drifting and thinning until only the moon lane is
   left on the water.
   Caption: *only smoke remembers*
