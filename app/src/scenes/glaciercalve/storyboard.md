# Storyboard — glaciercalve (20s)

Spatial axis: camera faces the glacier front; the calving slab lives at uv x≈0.55 (center-right). All action stays on the same side of the axis — the wall never crosses behind camera.

| Window | Shot | Content | Eye anchor |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–7s | **ACT 1 — THE FRONT** | Wide establishing: pale dusk sky, low sun wafer, slate sea; the serried ice wall fills midframe. Veins deepened toward the waterline shimmer faintly. | Wall silhouette, center. |
| 7–9.5s | **THE CRACK** | Camera pans right; a luminous blue fracture spiders across the slab region — segments ignite in sequence from the top edge down to the waterline. | Glowing crack, center-right. |
| 9.5–12s | **THE CALVE** | Slab drops on a slump envelope with ~12° of torque; the leading edge digs in. Dust-blue mist bursts at the waterline. | Falling block. |
| 11.3–14s | **IMPACT + SPLASH** | White foam crown erupts and collapses; concentric swell rings expand across the sea. ~12 berg shards surface; ~18 seabirds scatter once from the splash site. | Splash crown → rings. |
| 14–20s | **AFTERMATH** | Bergs bob on decaying sines and drift outward; glitter mist settles over the impact zone; wall face shows a new scar where the slab left. | Bergs + new scar. |

Captions (project.json): `01 THE FRONT`, `02 THE CALVE`, `03 AFTERMATH`.
