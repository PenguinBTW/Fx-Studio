# foxglovedell — storyboard (20 s)

| # | t (s) | act | on screen |
|---|-------|-----|-----------|
| 01 | 0–2.5 | STILL CLEARING | Dark canopy rim closes the frame; pale sky only through small top gaps; sunlit grass clearing at center ringed by seven foxglove spikes (pink-purple speckled bells); mossy log low-left, fern undergrowth right; sun shaft already angling onto the lit spike; dapple drifting on the grass. |
| 02 | 2.5–9 | WREN PATROL | The wren silhouette works along the log — bobbing steps, tail flicking — hops in a small arc to a foxglove base at mid-clearing, then works back left; spikes sway on separate phases; motes drift in the shaft. |
| 03 | 9–12.5 | THE SONG | The wren stops near mid-log, head and beak tilt up, beak opens, throat feathers puff — one held song; camera leans in a hair; dapple keeps sliding. |
| 04 | 12.5–18 | BEE VISIT | Two bees work up the lit spike, wobbling around the stem with wing shimmer; the first bee reaches the top bell, tucks into its mouth and vanishes inside; the fallen flower lies at the clearing edge. |
| 05 | 18–20 | SETTLE | Bees fade off; only the breeze on the spikes, the drifting motes and the slow dapple remain; the dell settles back to stillness. |
