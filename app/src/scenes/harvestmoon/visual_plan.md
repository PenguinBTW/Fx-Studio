# harvestmoon — visual plan

A huge orange harvest moon rising over a stubble field at blue hour. Macro-flat
silhouette staging like the sibling night scenes: a shader backdrop (sky, glow,
field) behind a z=-2 life layer of flat ribbon/polygon silhouettes.

## Composition (world coords, z=-2 life plane)

- Moon: shader disc dominating upper-mid frame, centered ~(0.55, y) rising
  0.32 → 0.95 across the film. Procedural craters + warm chromatic limb.
- Sky: deep blue-hour gradient; warm glow band low behind the moon's side.
- Field: dark stubble with faint row stripes catching moonlight near the
  horizon; warm spill on the tips directly under the moon.
- Left: low barn roofline silhouette (x ≈ -1.7).
- Right: one bare oak silhouette (x ≈ 1.9), branches clawing into frame.
- Middle: fence posts marching from mid-frame down toward bottom-center —
  a perspective rhythm the eye follows to the horizon.
- Far right horizon: one house window glow (x ≈ 2.3).

## Cast

- Combine silhouette far on the horizon (x ≈ -0.6); cab light pulses once ~14s.
- Hare bounding once along a stubble row, t 5–9s (three hop parabolas).
- Scarecrow at the fence line (x ≈ 0.9); tattered sleeve stirring in breeze.
- Two crow crossings over the moon's face: t 8.5–11.5 and 16.5–19.5 — a
  straggling line of 4–5 shapes with wing-beat flap, tied to the live moon pos.
- Fireflies low on the hedge behind the scarecrow from ~10s.
- House window warms on from ~12s.

## Beats

still field + rising moon → hare run → first crow cross → combine cab light →
second crow cross → settle.

## Camera

56mm, near-static, very low; slight lateral drift right so the moonrise
parallaxes against the barn/oak frame.
