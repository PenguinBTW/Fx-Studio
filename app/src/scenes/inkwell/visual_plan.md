# inkwell — visual plan

Diffusion register: entropy made beautiful. A drop of ink in water is the canonical image of disorder spreading — but the transition is pure lace. The catalog has fluid as material (mercury) but nothing about mixing itself — the interface where order dissolves.


## mapping table
| abstract concept | physical visual |
|---|---|
| entropy / diffusion | veining spreading until the water itself is ink |
| contamination / influence | darkness blooming through light |
| turbulence | domain-warped curl structure in the veil edges |
| time | the plume's descent rate, then the slowing spread |

## anti-cliché audit
- NOT a stock-footage ink drop — the veining must have genuine fluid-curl structure, not blobby noise
- no color ink — black ink in luminous water, the classical register
- no tank edges visible — the water is a world, not an aquarium

## focal hierarchy
1. the vein structure — the lace edges where ink meets water
2. the plume head during descent — the event
3. the backlight dimming through growing density
4. surface glint — establishing stillness, then gone

## light
Strong even backlight (the water glows), no key — the ink reads as near-black silhouette veining against luminous teal-white water. As density grows the backlight is swallowed — exposure falls gently.
