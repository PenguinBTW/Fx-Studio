# paperfold — visual plan

Transformation made physical: flatness becoming flight. The register the catalog lacks — topology change, paper as a material with memory. Anti-fantasist honesty: the folds are real kinematic rotations about real crease lines, not a morph.


## mapping table
| abstract concept | physical visual |
|---|---|
| potential energy / latency | the flat sheet's stillness — light raking across grain |
| transformation | kinematic fold rotations, crease shadows deepening |
| intention / design | the precision of edges meeting the centerline |
| release / freedom | liftoff — the only non-fold motion in the piece, banked glide |
| fragility | paper thinness read at edges against backlight; slight tremor before flight |

## anti-cliché audit
- NOT a stop-motion paper craft video — continuous kinematics, no frame-stepping feel
- no hands, no human presence — the sheet folds itself, the magic is in the material
- no origami crane (overused icon) — the dart airplane, humbler and more aerodynamic in spirit
- no celebratory golden light at completion — the plane leaves into darkness, ambiguity intact

## focal hierarchy
1. the active fold — whichever crease is moving (brightest, sharpest)
2. paper grain and edge thinness — materiality second
3. the dart silhouette at rest — the reward shape
4. the glide — small in frame, diminishing, final

## light
Single warm tungsten key (3200K, spectral) high left, dim cool fill; dark void backdrop. Crease shadows carry the fold readability. During flight the key light dims slightly — the plane glides into the dark.
