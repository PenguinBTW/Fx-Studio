# storyboard — koipond (20s)

- **00.0–05.0 — GLASS**: The pond is a mirror. Teal centre, warm sky caught
  at the edges; willow fronds hang over the frame border. Lily pads and
  lotus blossoms ride a long slow swell; koi shadows cruise beneath.
  A willow drop lands occasionally — one clean ring.
- **05.0–10.0 — SURFACING**: The koi rise. Orange/white/black bands bloom
  through the water; bodies undulate on S-curve tail waves along wandering
  paths. A fish touches the surface — kiss ring, tiny glint. The camera
  begins a gentle drift after the largest koi.
- **10.0–13.0 — THE PLOP**: A frog silhouette sits on a pad; the pad dips
  under its weight. At ~11.3 the frog launches off-frame and a fat plop
  ring spreads where the pad was. Dragonflies begin skimming the surface.
- **14.0–16.0 — THE LEAP**: The dusk gold deepens. The largest koi arcs
  briefly out of the water (~14.8–15.8) and comes down with a splash ring.
- **15.5–20.0 — REVEAL**: The camera pulls back — the whole pond holds:
  pads, rings fading, petals drifting, gold deepening toward night.
