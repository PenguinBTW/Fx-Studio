# Storyboard — sandstorm (20s, 3 acts)

## Act 1 — THE WALL ON THE HORIZON (0–8s)
- Wide establishing from mid-dune. Heat shimmer waves the horizon; two acacia silhouettes and a dead tree anchor scale. Sun high and hot.
- The haboob reads as a distant amber band that thickens and lifts — lobes forming at the crown.
- Camera: slow push-in, breath drift. Gaze anchored center-right on the wall's leading face.

## Act 2 — CONTACT (8–15s)
- The wall towers — billowing lobes roll over the crown, interior fbm puffs churn.
- Sun collapses to a dim red wafer; foreground grain streaks rip past lens; tumbleweed crosses on tumble arcs.
- Visibility collapses into near-whiteout at ~13–15s — pale amber field, streaks at max violence, brief dead-calm lull at the deepest opacity.

## Act 3 — THE SETTLE (15–20s)
- Back of the front passes; visibility re-emerges through golden haze. Sun returns as a pale wafer.
- The dune ridgeline silhouette is subtly reshaped (new crest phase). Grains die down to scattered ticks.
- Camera settles to a quiet hold on the transformed plain.
