# Visual Architecture Plan

**Chapter / Segment:** Sandstorm — haboob wall advancing across a Sahara plain
**Core Abstract Concept:** An unstoppable approaching force that swallows scale (a landscape-level weather event)
**Primary Physical Domain:** Fluid/Topographic — a towering dust density front over a rippled sand plain

---

## 1. The Conceptual Mapping Table

| Script Abstract Concept | Real-World Physical Metaphor | Tangible Form & Materials | Dynamic Visual State Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| *The approaching front* | Haboob density wall | Amber fbm billow mass, lobed crown rim-lit on top | Wall advances 0→13s: thin horizon band → frame-filling tower → whiteout |
| *Sun extinction* | Dimming red wafer | Hot disc occluded by dust optical depth | Disc fades 8→13s to a dull red coin, gone at whiteout, returns as pale gold |
| *Wind violence at the lens* | Saltation streaks | Anisotropic instanced grain smears crossing frame | Density/velocity ramps with wall proximity; peak chaos inside the whiteout |
| *Loose debris* | Tumbleweed arcs | Dry bush bodies on ballistic closed-form tumble paths | Cross frame on hops during the contact window |
| *Aftermath* | Settling golden haze | Suspended fine dust, reshaped dune ridgeline | Visibility returns through amber fog; front crest silhouette has moved |

---

## 2. Anti-Cliché Audit
- **Forbidden:** sepia photo filter + generic wind lines. **Replacement:** true volumetric-feeling fbm density with crown lobes and optical-depth sun extinction.
- **Forbidden:** cartoon spiral tornado shape. **Replacement:** flat-front advancing wall (haboob morphology — blunt leading face, billowing crown, ground skirt).

## 3. Spatial Hierarchy & Eye-Tracking
- **Hero (60%):** the dust wall's leading face + lobed crown, center-right, grows until it dominates.
- **Secondary (30%):** sun disc + acacia silhouettes establishing scale, foreground dune face.
- **Ambient (10%):** heat shimmer band on the horizon, saltation streaks, ground haze.
