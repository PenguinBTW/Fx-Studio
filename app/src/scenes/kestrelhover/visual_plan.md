# Kestrel Hover — visual plan

A single kestrel hovering head-to-wind over a chalk hillside verge: the bird is
a fixed point while grass, gusts, dust, a skylark and its own stoop all move.

## Establishing
- Rolling chalk hillside (low sine ridge), distant hedge line at y≈0.40, pale
  noon sky with thin drifting cloud streaks and a faint sun halo upper-left.
- Dry grass verge mid-frame (y≈0.18–0.42): blades hashed from a leaning ramp,
  lean driven by wind — ambient drift plus the big gust envelope.

## Hero — the kestrel
- Silhouette at ~(0.52, 0.63), ~0.03 uv wingspan: tilted body ellipse, fanned
  tail, head pitched down scanning; two-segment wings whose tips ride a ~4.3 Hz
  flap with a second harmonic.
- Tiny bob (3–4 ‰ of frame). Two drop-and-rise dips (4s, 11s) — a few feet
  down, re-rise over ~1.5s.
- Stoop at ~13s: wings fold to a V, body pitches 60°+, and it dives off the
  bottom-right over ~0.85s. It glides back in from upper-left over ~1.6s and
  resumes the hover.

## Wind & life
- Gust at ~5–8s: grass lean doubles, one straw fragment tears loose and is
  carried across frame, dust puff rolls along the hedge.
- Skylark pops from the verge at ~9s — vertical rise, flutters, levels off and
  exits right by ~12s.
- Rabbit ears flash once in the grass (~6.4s).

## Camera
- Very low, near-static: a 1.6-unit lateral drift along the hillside line plus
  a 0.6-unit push during the stoop. Kestrel holds compositional center.
