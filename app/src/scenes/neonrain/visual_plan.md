# neonrain — visual plan

Don't forget to ground yourself when its time USE THE SKILL and REREAD IT ALL

Rain-slicked city alley at night, ~20 s, fully procedural, closed-form f(t).

## World
- Canyon: hashed storefront facades crowd both frame edges, lit windows sparse; overhead cables sag across the dark sky.
- Wet asphalt: real ground plane — dark sheen, mirrored neon reflection columns smeared down the alley (wobble-distorted), curb-level mist, expanding splash arcs.
- Signs: 5 neon signs on the facades — cyan/magenta/gold/pink/blue, seeded; soft bloom discs; sign #3 flickers in bursts.
- Rain: two depth layers of hashed diagonal streaks (near fast/long, far slow/short).
- Life: two umbrella silhouettes cross at different depths; a doorway light opens and closes once far down the alley.

## Camera
- CameraRig slow push down the alley (z +24 → +9), faint roll drift; holds center on the vanishing point.

## Beats
1. t 0–7: rain builds, signs hum awake.
2. t 8–12: sign flicker event — stutters, reflections shiver.
3. t 12–16: passersby cross; doorway light opens.
4. t 16–20: storm eases, reflections lengthen.
