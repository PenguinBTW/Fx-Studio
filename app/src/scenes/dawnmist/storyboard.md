# dawnmist — storyboard

Entry `dawnmist/mist_rower`, t = 0–20.

## Shots

1. **0–2.5 — Grey holds the lake.** Establishing frame: three hill ridges
   recede into a muted violet sky; a pale moon remnant hangs left. Mist
   banks obscure the water's surface; reeds stand dark on the near edge.
   Camera nearly still, slow drift.
   Caption: *grey holds the lake*

2. **3.2 — First rings.** A fish rises mid-water: a ring spreads on the
   calm surface, marking the first life.

3. **4–7 — Gold arrives.** The sun clears the ridge: horizon burns
   pink-gold, a glint lane develops on the water and widens toward the
   camera. The mist warms from grey to gold.
   Caption: *first light on the water*

4. **6–15 — The crossing.** The rowing boat enters right and crosses on a
   steady path; oars dip on a stroke cycle, each dip spreading a small
   ring, the hull trailing a two-line wake. Camera drifts laterally with it.

5. **14.5–18 — Duck vee.** Five ducks cross in a shallow vee through the
   parting mist, each trailing a small wake.

6. **15–19 — The reveal.** Mist banks thin and burn off; the far-shore
   treeline resolves against the brightening sky. A second fish rings the
   now-clear water. The boat exits left.
   Caption: *the mist gives it back*
