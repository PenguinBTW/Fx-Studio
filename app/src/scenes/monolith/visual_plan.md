# monolith — visual plan

## Concept
The word GRAVITY as massive extruded letterforms in a dark studio void. Letters
slam down one at a time with physical weight — each impact rings the floor with
a shockwave of dust — then light rakes slowly across the type while the camera
drifts in a low arc. Typography as sculpture, mass as meaning.

## Register gap
Catalog: naturalistic, macro, architectural, abstract. Missing: TYPOGRAPHIC.
The engine's in-world 3D PBR extruded type (text_lighting.ts) is built for
exactly this and unused by the catalog: real shadows, thin-lens DoF, per-glyph
kinematics.

## Style guidance applied
- forge lesson: type must not become sci-fi HUD — no glow grids, no scanlines;
  monolithic letterforms in near-black bronze, one hard light, long shadows.
- brookside lesson: everything belongs — type, dust, floor, light are one studio.
- viopls art direction: heavy weight for motion legibility.

## Palette / light
- Near-black void, warm key spotlight (slight amber) raking across letters.
- Letterforms: dark bronze, clearcoat edge response, bevel highlights.
- Dust motes warm in the key beam.

## Movement
- t 0–8: letters slam down L→R, one per second — ease-in drop, hard stop,
  shockwave ring + dust burst per impact, camera tremor on each hit (trauma).
- t 8–30: settle. Camera drifts in a slow low arc; the key light sweeps
  azimuth so shadows crawl across the letterforms. Dust drifts through the beam.
