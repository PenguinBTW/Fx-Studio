# Sequence Storyboard: CHLADNI

**Target Duration:** 22.5s, single continuous scene (4 internal beats)
**Core Metaphor System:** Driven steel Chladni plate; salt grains reveal nodal figures
**Primary Motion Axis:** Slow counter-clockwise orbit (camera stays one side of the drive column axis the whole scene)

---

## Shot Sequence Breakdown

### Beat A: The Quiet Plate (scatter state)
- **Window:** 0.0 – 3.5s
- **Shot Scale & Lens:** Establishing wide | ~35mm equivalent | 3/4 elevated angle
- **Camera Vector:** Slow orbital drift + gentle dolly-in; never locks
- **Framing & Action:** Rig revealed out of darkness; grains lie as random noise; drive readout idles at STANDBY
- **Focal Exit Point:** Screen centre (960, 560) on the grain field

### Beat B: Mode (1,2) — first figure
- **Window:** 3.5 – 9.0s (step at 3.5, settled by ~5.3)
- **Shot Scale & Lens:** Medium context | 50mm
- **Camera Vector:** Orbit continues, pushes to lower elevation, tighter radius
- **Framing & Action:** Signal pulse ring sweeps the rim; grains jitter-lift then migrate to nodal lines; readout rolls to 482 Hz
- **Focal Entry/Exit:** Centre — figure forms under the viewer's gaze
- **Caption:** `MODE (1,2) · 482 HZ`

### Beat C: Mode (2,3) — the re-sort
- **Window:** 9.0 – 15.0s (step at 9.0)
- **Shot Scale & Lens:** Macro close | ~85mm | shallow focus
- **Camera Vector:** Low sweep along the plate plane, grazing a nodal line as grains stream in
- **Framing & Action:** Kick of tremor, grains re-form into the more complex (2,3) figure; readout rolls to 1097 Hz
- **Caption:** `MODE (2,3) · 1097 HZ`

### Beat D: Mode (3,4) — hero reveal
- **Window:** 15.0 – 22.5s (step at 15.0)
- **Shot Scale & Lens:** Pull-back to elevated hero | 40mm
- **Camera Vector:** Crane-up + pull-out to a near-plan view of the full plate
- **Framing & Action:** The densest figure assembles; final rim pulse at ~18.7s; readout rolls to 2052 Hz; hold-creep to cut
- **Caption:** `MODE (3,4) · 2052 HZ`

## Seam & Continuity Notes
- Single scene — no cuts; momentum preserved by one unbroken orbital direction.
- Each step pulses the rim + tremor burst so every re-form reads as a distinct "beat" without a cut.
