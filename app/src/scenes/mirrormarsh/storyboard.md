# mirrormarsh — storyboard

20 s single take, camera near-static (±0.5 u drift), 16:9.

**0.0–3.5 — establish.** Wide marsh: peach→blue dawn sky mirrored
seamlessly, reed banks doubled in still water, mist bank behind, rowboat
far right. Caption: "a marsh holding its breath".

**3.5–7.5 — first mist.** Low bands start peeling left→right across the
water; the mirror quivers only inside the moving bands. One distant
fish-rise ring opens far left (~4.5 s). The heron's head dips once — a
peek, not a strike. Caption: "mist begins to peel across the water".

**7.6–10.5 — the strike.** The heron's neck pitches down at ~8.2 s; beak
hits the water, splash ring expands, a slower drip ring follows. Frog
bubbles cluster near x≈0.33 around 10–11 s. Caption: "one strike — then
stillness again".

**12.3–14.5 — kingfisher.** A teal streak darts in from the right
(~12.3–12.8), perches on a reed tip as a bright orange-bellied speck
(~12.8–14.3), then dives and streaks out low. Caption: "a kingfisher
comes and goes".

**15.8–20 — settle.** A second dragonfly crosses (~16.2). Mist thins;
the mirror closes back to glass. Caption: "the mirror returns to
itself". Fade on the level ramp.
