# atrium — storyboard

| t (s) | beat |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Open low, in shade: the staircase underside fills frame, shaft a bright column beyond. |
| 5–14 | Rise begins — treads sweep past in parallax as the oculus reveals itself overhead. |
| 14–22 | Mid-ascent: camera drifts toward the shaft; dust motes bloom large in soft focus; concrete bands rhythm across frame. |
| 22–30 | Near the top the shaft widens, the oculus ring dominates; camera settles facing into the light as dust swirls. |
