# atrium — visual plan

## Concept
A brutalist concrete spiral atrium interior. Sun enters through a circular oculus
high above and descends the void as a slow-moving shaft; dust drifts through it.
The camera rises gently along the helix, never cutting, always motivated by the
light. Architecture as the subject — no people, no furniture, no clutter.

## Register gap
Every catalog scene is naturalistic landscape/macro (fernhollow). The missing
register is ARCHITECTURAL: hard-surface PBR, negative space, light as a physical
material. Distinct from cathedraldust (outdoor ruin, vegetation, narrative debris)
— this is interior, intact, minimal, abstracted.

## Style guidance applied
- forge lesson: minimalism must stay warm and material — no chrome, no sci-fi;
  board-formed concrete texture, warm sun vs cool shade, nothing synthetic.
- brookside lesson: every element belongs — stair, columns, oculus, dust are one
  coherent building.
- Terrest lesson: slow motivated camera only.

## Palette / light
- Warm concrete #b8a98f in sun, cool shadow #4a4640.
- Shaft: warm white #fff3dd additive volumes.
- Deep shade floor #2a2620.

## Movement
- Camera: slow helical rise, 24s for one half-turn, drift toward the shaft then away.
- Dust: analytical particles in the shaft volume.
- Nothing else moves — the architecture and light carry the scene.
