# fulmen — visual plan

Register: electrical/atmospheric-discharge — light as plasma. The catalog has fire (kindling) and weather volume (cloudfall) but no electricity.

Subject: a night thunderhead over dark terrain. Sheet lightning flickers inside the cloud volume; stepped leaders probe down; return strokes strike with re-strikes through the same channel (real lightning physics: most flashes are 3-4 strokes).

Concept anchors:
- darkness as default: the scene is black except when plasma exists — exposure rides the flash envelope
- re-strike realism: dart leaders reuse the ionized channel; each strike fires 1-3 restrikes
- the rain shaft is only visible when light catches it

Palette: near-black blue night; violet-white plasma; cool flash-lit terrain edges.
Camera: slow push-in at storm scale, wide and low — the bolt must dwarf the frame.
