# cathedraldust — storyboard

20 s single take. The camera never cuts; the story is the light.

| t | beat | what the frame holds |
|---|------|----------------------|
| 0–4 | still beams | dark nave, 3 gold shafts through the vault breach, motes crossing them, caption "the nave holds its breath" |
| 4–9 | drift | motes keep falling in both bands, chain begins to sway, caption "two hundred motes own the light" |
| 9–13.4 | pigeon | silhouette leaves the left rafters, crosses beam 1, loops once, lands right — caption "one pigeon crosses the beam" |
| 13–18 | the sun shifts | floor intercepts slide right, beam 2 thins out as beam 3 enters, colored glass spots crawl, a fragment drops and puffs at the floor — caption "the sun shifts its weight" |
| 18–20 | settle | motes keep falling, beams hold their new rake, caption "stone keeps what light forgets" |

Captions anchor mid-frame, projection-clamped to stay inside canvas.
Post: low exposure, gentle bloom threshold 0.70 so beams glow, warm
grain 0.06, vignette 0.45.
