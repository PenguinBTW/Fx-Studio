# biolume — visual plan

Register: aquatic / bioluminescent — the catalog's first deep-sea scene. Every photon is organism-made; there is no sun.

Subject: a lone jellyfish pulsing up through a dark water column, trailing tentacles, provoking flash cascades in the plankton field as the pressure wave of each bell contraction passes. Marine snow sifts downward throughout — the deep ocean's constant weather.

Concept anchors:
- light as biology: the bell's cyan core, violet gonad ring, plankton flashes — nothing is lit from outside
- pressure made visible: the flash cascade is the jellyfish's wake written in plankton
- verticality: snow falls, the jelly rises — the column is the composition

Palette: abyssal blue-black ground; cyan/teal bioluminescence; violet organ accents.
Camera: slow drifting orbit at jelly depth, macro-intimate, DoF on the bell.
