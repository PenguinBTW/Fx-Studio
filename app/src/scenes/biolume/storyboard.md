# biolume — storyboard (30s continuous)

- 0–4s: dark water column, marine snow, sparse spontaneous plankton flashes. The jellyfish drifts up from below frame.
- 4–14s: bell contractions begin — visible pulse cycle, flash cascades ring outward through the plankton at each squeeze.
- 14–24s: steady ascent, tentacles trailing with traveling sway waves, camera arcing slowly.
- 24–30s: the jelly rises toward the faint photic zone; cascade activity continues; snow falls on.
