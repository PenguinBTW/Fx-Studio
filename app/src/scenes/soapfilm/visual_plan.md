# soapfilm — visual plan

Register: wave-optics/iridescence. optica decomposes light by refraction; soapfilm decomposes it by interference — color from film geometry in nanometers, no pigment anywhere.

Subject: a soap bubble floating in a dim studio void. The film drains under gravity (thinner at top, thickening toward the rim), convection marbles the thickness field, and the interference bands swirl accordingly. At 19s the film drains critically — the bubble pops into a droplet mist. A second bubble is blown after, smaller, rising.

Concept anchors:
- color as physics: every hue is a computed wavelength interaction, not a palette choice
- mortality as structure: the drainage clock IS the narrative — the pop is written into the film from the first frame
- near-invisibility: the bubble is mostly not there — it exists at grazing angles and highlights

Palette: charcoal studio void; the entire spectrum, physically derived.
Camera: intimate slow orbit, widening for the pop, DoF on the film surface.


## lighting
dim studio void — hemisphere fill only; the bubble is self-revealing through interference and two implied soft-box reflections (streak highlights computed in-shader).

## composition
single-subject macro intimacy; the bubble off-center-left during drift, centering for the pop beat, second bubble entering below-right for asymmetric rebirth balance.

## motion language
everything slow and buoyant — drift amplitudes in centimeters; violence only in the 0.5s pop.
