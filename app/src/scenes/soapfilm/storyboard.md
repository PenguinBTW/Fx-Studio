# soapfilm — storyboard (30s continuous)

- 0–1.2s: the bubble scales in out of the void
- 1.2–12s: floating drift, interference marbling evolving as the film drains
- 12–19s: drainage accelerates visibly — top of the film thinning toward black-invisible
- 19s: POP — holes spread across the film in half a second, droplet mist bursts outward
- 19–21.5s: mist settles; the void empty
- 21.5–24.5s: a second bubble is blown — scales in below-right
- 24.5–30s: the new bubble rises, fresh thick film marbling, camera settling
