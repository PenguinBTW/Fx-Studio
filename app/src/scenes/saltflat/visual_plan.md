# saltflat — visual plan

A vast cracked salt flat at dawn under a lavender sky. One tiny explorer walks
left, trailing a kite-string of light that unspools up to a small diamond kite.

## Composition (authored uv, x→right, y→up)

- y 0.0–0.30: near-field salt crust — voronoi polygon cracks growing toward the
  bottom edge (fake perspective), a few seeded crystal glints.
- y 0.30–0.62: mid flat fading to the thin white horizon band; mirage
  heat-shimmer wobble hugging y ~0.55–0.62; salt-devil spiral stage right.
- y 0.62–1.0: lavender→peach dawn gradient; sun disc just clearing the horizon
  at x≈0.30; two mountain silhouettes; bird flock crosses the pink band once.
- Walker at y≈0.38, ~0.02 tall, starts x=0.62 and walks left to x≈0.42.
- Thread: from the walker's hand back-right-up to the kite at (0.66, 0.80).

## Light

Dawn backlight: everything on the flat is near-silhouette except the thread,
the kite flash, the glints and the sun itself. Post bloom carries the flare.

## Motion beats (20s)

1. 0–3 STILL FLAT: salt crust, sun rising a touch, shimmer idle.
2. 3–9 THE WALKER: walker enters pace, thread unspools behind; hare lifts
   head ~7s and bounds off right.
3. 9–12 KITE FLASH: kite catches the sun once (~10s) — hard flash + rack.
4. 12–17 SALT-DEVIL: dust spiral spins up ~14s, collapses ~16.5s.
5. 17–20 SETTLE: birds cross the pink band, thread stills, deep dawn.
