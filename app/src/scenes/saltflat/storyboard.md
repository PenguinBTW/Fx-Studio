- t3-9   THE WALKER — silhouette paces left, glowing thread unspools behind;
         vicuña lifts its head ~7s, bounds off.
- t9-12  KITE FLASH — kite catches the sun once (~10s); subtle rack to it.
- t12-17 SALT-DEVIL — dust spiral spins up right-of-center and collapses.
- t17-20 SETTLE — far birds cross the pink band; the flat holds still.

Camera: low, slow leftward track matching the walker's pace; a brief rack/
push when the kite flashes. Probes: "walker" on the figure, "kite" on the
diamond.
