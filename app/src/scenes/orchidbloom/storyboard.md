# orchidbloom — storyboard

Five acts, captions lowercase probe style as in the brief.

1. **act-closed** (0–4.8): "BUDS CLOSED" — dark moss backdrop, stem with four
   green buds, shafts beginning to slide, millipede crossing the mound.
2. **act-sepals** (4.8–9.6): "SEPALS SPLIT" — hero bud's three sepals peel
   back in stages, exposing the white-pink folded petals.
3. **act-petals** (9.6–14.4): "PETALS OPEN" — petals unfurl to the open face;
   the spotted lip flares out; a neighboring bud stirs.
4. **act-flies** (14.4–19.2): "FIRST HOVER-FLY" — two hover-flies circle the
   bloom and land on the lip; a spider thread slides past frame right.
5. **act-droplet** (19.2–24): "DROPLET DROP" — a dew bead grows on the petal
   edge, slides, and falls; sun glint sweeps the lip; the bloom settles.
