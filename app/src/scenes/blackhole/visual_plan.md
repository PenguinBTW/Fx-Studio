# blackhole — visual plan

Concept: a lensed accretion disk — light made to fall around a dark sphere.

- **Staging**: black sphere (event horizon) with a thin fresnel photon-ring; the disk is a flat ring band in the x-z plane tilted ~12°; the lensing warp is analytic — the disk's far side (−z, behind the sphere) is re-emitted as a second ring band bent over the top, plus a dimmer ghost band underneath. No raytracer.
- **Color language**: doppler beaming — +x side blue-white and boosted, −x side amber and dim; inner-edge plasma burns near-white regardless of side. Azimuthal fbm streaks shear via differential rotation ω(r).
- **Beat 1 — THE SILENT LENS (t 0–8)**: dim disk, slow push-in; silhouette + photon ring carry the frame.
- **Beat 2 — THE DISK BRIGHTENS (t 8–16)**: `uBright` ramps; streaks, doppler asymmetry and the over-top arc become legible.
- **Beat 3 — THE WARP REVEALS (t 16–24)**: camera dips toward the disk plane (parallax on approach), revealing the upper arc / under-arc ghost geometry.
- All motion closed-form `f(t)`; per-ctx state via WeakMap; star field bends inward near the limb (cheap lens distortion).
