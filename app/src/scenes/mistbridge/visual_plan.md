# mistbridge — visual plan

A narrow stone footbridge over a deep gorge at dawn. Thin mist fills the gorge
bottom; a lone hooded traveler crosses slowly, pauses mid-span to look down,
then stops at the far end and looks back once.

## Frame

Near-static low shot, 58mm lens, camera slightly below deck height looking
level — the bridge reads as a thin pale line against the drop.

- **Top third:** apricot→steel dawn sky; a distant sun band kisses only the
  upper rock rim late in the film.
- **Middle:** the footbridge spans the gap at mid-frame; gorge walls plunge on
  left and right, fading into white mist; a far ledge right holds a leaning
  dead pine and a goat.
- **Bottom:** layered mist banks drift slowly through the gorge; wisps curl
  up around the parapets; two birds glide through the gap on thermals.

## Palette

- Sky: pale steel `#5a6670` → apricot `#e8b890` at the horizon remnant.
- Rock: cold grey `#3a4048` lightening into mist.
- Mist: layered whites `0.75–0.92` alpha-sculpted, warm at top, cool below.
- Bridge stone: pale grey `#8a8578` with a warm glint along the crown edge.
- Traveler/goat/raven/birds/pine: near-black silhouettes.

## Motion language

Everything is silhouette-and-mist: shapes read by outline against the pale
drop. Motion is slow and weighty — the traveler's measured steps, the mist's
two-band drift, one parabolic goat hop, one raven circle, one stone that
falls and is swallowed. The only "event" is the crossing itself.

## Beats → acts

1. **still gorge** (0–4): mist breathing, first birds.
2. **a traveler** (4–10): figure enters left, measured steps.
3. **mid-span** (10–13.5): pause, lean, look down; stone falls.
4. **across** (13.5–18): resumes, raven circles the pine, sun band lights.
5. **look back** (18–24): stops at the far end, turns once, settles.
