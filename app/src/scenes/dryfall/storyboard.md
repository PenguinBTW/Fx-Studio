# dryfall — storyboard

## i — NOON LEDGE (0–3.5)

Empty frame: sandstone face, dark dry-fall stain, the log across the gap.
Three ravens circle the rim at different radii. Pigeon bobbing far right.
Heat shimmer over the canyon floor. The scene breathes before anyone crosses.

## ii — THE CROSSING (3.5–9.6)

A hiker silhouette walks in along the left shelf, steps onto the log, and
crosses with his balance arm held out. Halfway across he pitches forward —
arm windmills, knee dips — recovers, keeps going, steps onto the far shelf
and walks off frame right.

## iii — RAVEN ON THE LOG (9.6–13)

The log sits empty again — then raven 2 peels out of its orbit, drops to the
log's right end, folds its wings, sits a beat, and lifts back into the circle.

## iv — FLOOR LIFE (13–16.4)

Down on the gravel: a lizard dart-freeze-dart across the left shelf, a
tumbleweed rolling through the bottom of frame with little hops, and a dust
devil wisp corkscrewing across the canyon floor.

## v — SETTLE (16.4–19)

Back to stillness: ravens circling, pigeon on the rim, shimmer on the floor.
The hiker is gone; the log waits.
