# harborfog — storyboard

| Act | t | Caption | Action |
|-----|---|---------|--------|
| i | 0–4.8 | FOG THICK | Two fog bands drift at different speeds, half-swallowing the moored hulls; breakwater and lighthouse tower silhouettes at the back |
| ii | 4.8–9.6 | FIRST LAMPS | Deck lamps warm on hull by hull — warm cores + halos + water smears; the mooring buoy starts its slow red blink |
| iii | 9.6–14.4 | BEAM SWEEPS | The lighthouse beam crosses the harbor, flaring the fog band in its path; a gull is caught on its post for a beat; beam dust glints |
| iv | 14.4–19.2 | CORMORANT — FOGHORN FAR OFF | A cormorant streaks low left→right over the water; two ripple rings spread where something surfaces |
| v | 19.2–24 | SWELL SETTLES | Mast roll damps out, lamp smears lengthen in the calm; a final low beam pass lays a glint lane on the water |
