# emberdrift — storyboard

## 01 THE CLEARING (0–6s)

- Wide on the clearing: pine walls frame both edges, moon wafer
  upper-left, haze band low.
- Campfire at bottom-center: coal bed pulses, three flame tongues sway,
  log silhouette; light pool on the forest floor.
- Ember stream starts thin — a dozen points climbing on slow helices,
  flash-bright near the fire, dimming as they rise.
- Camera: held wide with breath drift.

## 02 THE SWELL (6–9.6s)

- A gust rolls through: the haze band leans, flame tongues tip right,
  the ember stream kinks and accelerates sideways.
- Gust-caught embers (the seeded subset) surge upward in brighter
  bursts; the stream column widens.
- Camera: still wide, drift continues — the swell is in the air, not
  the lens.

## 03 THE CRACK (9.6–12.5s)

- The log cracks: a fountain of fresh embers ejects upward, the coal
  bed flares, two new flame tongues flash on.
- Camera: micro-shake impulse (decaying sine), then a slow push
  toward the fire — the first time the frame moves with intent.
- Moths draw in, entering their orbit of the light cone.

## 04 THE DRIFT (12.5–20s)

- The fountain exhausts; the stream returns to a slow drift but the
  clearing holds more haze and the fire burns brighter.
- Moths orbit the light cone once — dark flutter silhouettes against
  the ember column.
- Embers climb into the moon band and fade to ash.
- Camera eases back a touch; fade out on the persistent ember stream.
