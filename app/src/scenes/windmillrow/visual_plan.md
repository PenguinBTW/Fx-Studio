# windmillrow — visual plan

Three old windmills turning over golden farmland at last light. Single
20 s take, no cuts.

## Palette

- Sky: warm gold → rose → indigo dusk bands, desaturating blue as the
  light dies (warm→cool arc over t 13–18.5).
- Sun: low disc at the right horizon edge, amber glow, dimming late.
- Field: wheat golds (row-band alternation), hazier toward the ridge.
- Mills/birds/hare/crow: near-black silhouettes; rotors get a warm rim
  when a blade crosses the sun lane.

## Composition

- Backdrop plane 320×140 at z=-80; camera at z≈24, fov 40° (visible uv
  window ≈ ±0.19 around the panned center).
- Ridge silhouette at uv y≈0.415; wheat rows below.
- Near mill at uv x 0.28 (partial frame left early), mid 0.55, far 0.78 —
  sizes 17/11/7.5 world for depth.
- CameraRig pans target.x +25 → -25 over t 1–14: sweeps the ridge then
  settles on the near mill.

## Motion beats (closed-form f(t))

- t 0–5: slow establish; rotors at ~45 % rate, field still.
- t 5–9: wind arrives — wheat waves sweep left→right, rotors spin up.
- t 8–13: seven birds cross once on a long diagonal.
- t 10–16: hare bounds through the near wheat row (bound-pause).
- t 13–18.5: dusk cooling; long shadows stretch across the field.
- t 15–18: crow rests on the near mill's tail vane.
- t 17–19.5: wind dies; rotors ease to near-still.

## Text

Three probe captions: "last light on the wheat" / "wind comes over the
ridge" / "the mills run hard before dark" — Archivo 500, knockout halo.
