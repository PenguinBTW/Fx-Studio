# fireflymarsh — storyboard

24 s · 16:9 · 60 fps · three 8-second acts, continuous CameraRig drift
along the waterline.

## Shot logic

- **t≈3:** afterglow strip, reed clumps swaying, first flashes pop.
- **t≈10:** chorus — dozens of cyan-gold pulses with synced water smears.
- **t≈15–17:** the still — every flash envelope gates to dark, marsh goes quiet.
- **t≈19:** frog plop — one ripple ring spreads across the water band,
  nearby firefly paths bend briefly.
- **t≈20–23:** owl silhouette glides across the sky.
- **t≈22:** hero fireflies cross close with bloom halos; rekindled chorus.

## Focal points

1. Waterline + reflections — the visual anchor of every act.
2. The dark beat — negative space is the point; hold it.
3. Hero crossing low-right — the closing texture.
