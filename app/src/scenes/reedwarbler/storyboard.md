# reedwarbler — storyboard (22s)

1. **NOON STILL (0–4.4s)** — dense reed bed across the top third, feathery
   tops over open water, sun glitter crawling on the pond. One fish-touch
   ring opens early. The floating leaf begins its slow circuit of the edge.
2. **WARBLER (4.4–9.6s)** — the sedge warbler darts in from the bed right,
   perches on a mid stem, calls once (beak opens, throat puffs), drops out
   of sight, reappears on a higher stem and dips to the reed base — feeding
   the nest.
3. **DRAGONFLY (9.6–14.6s)** — the dragonfly's oval patrol dominates the
   open water while a frog ripples the bank edge once. The warbler repeats:
   dart, perch, second nest-dip.
4. **MOORHEN (14.6–19.0s)** — a moorhen stalks the near bank left→right with
   a head-bob gait; a water boatman pulses to the surface; the hoverfly
   holds station over the bank.
5. **SETTLE (19.0–22s)** — the warbler returns to a high stem and sits; the
   leaf completes its circuit; glitter and reed sway carry the outro.
