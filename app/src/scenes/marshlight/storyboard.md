# marshlight — storyboard

| Act | t (s) | Shot | Action |
|-----|-------|------|--------|
| i DARK MARSH | 0–4 | Static wide | Dusk deepens over the marsh; stars prick in; the lamp warms, first faint sweep edge. |
| ii FIRST SWEEP | 4–8.5 | Wide | The beam sweeps left-to-right; grass tops flash; glint lines run down the creeks. |
| iii CURLEW | 8.5–13 | Wide, left channel | A curlew silhouette walks the near channel edge, pauses, probes twice; a seed head drifts off the grass. |
| iv SECOND SWEEP | 13–17.5 | Wide | The beam crosses again; a bat flickers past the lamp head once. |
| v SETTLE | 17.5–22 | Wide | A rabbit hops between the foreground clumps; the beam settles to a glow; stars hold. |
