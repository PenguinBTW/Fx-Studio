# marshlight — visual plan

A lighthouse on a marsh creek at deep twilight. One rotating beam sweeps the
marsh every ~6 s; winding creek channels catch it in quick glints; marram
grass sways in the foreground; a curlew works a channel edge.

## Composition (16:9)

- **Sky** — deep-dusk indigo→steel gradient, faint amber remnant at the west
  rim, a scatter of seeded first stars twinkling in.
- **Horizon** — low dune line silhouette across the back.
- **Lighthouse** — tall white tower on a small rise, right-of-center
  (x ≈ 1.35). Tapered shaft, dark gallery + lamp room, lamp glow halo.
- **Creeks** — 3 winding dark channels snaking through the marsh floor;
  each carries a thin glint ribbon that only lights when the beam's azimuth
  crosses it; the centre channel holds a slow run-off shimmer regardless.
- **Foreground** — 5 marram-grass clumps swaying; one close blade at frame
  left sways wide on occasional gusts.

## Beam

A wedge fanning from the lamp room, additive-blended, sweeping azimuth
-164°→-46° (left-to-right across the marsh floor) on a 6 s period with edge
dwells. A faint counter-beam. Grass tops and creek glints gate on the beam's
live azimuth — they flash only as the shaft passes their bearing.

## Life beats

- t≈9–13 — curlew silhouette walks a channel edge, pauses, probes twice.
- t≈12 — a grass seed head detaches and drifts off right.
- t≈16 — a bat flickers past the lamp once.
- t≈18–20 — a rabbit silhouette hops between foreground clumps.

## Camera

Very low, near-static; slight drift toward the lighthouse so grass parallax
reads. 56 mm.

## Beats / acts

1. DARK MARSH (0–4) — twilight settle, first stars, beam warms up.
2. FIRST SWEEP (4–8.5) — full left-to-right sweep, channel glints run.
3. CURLEW (8.5–13) — wading bird beat on the near channel.
4. SECOND SWEEP (13–17.5) — bat pass against the lamp.
5. SETTLE (17.5–22) — rabbit hop, beam fades to glow, stars hold.
