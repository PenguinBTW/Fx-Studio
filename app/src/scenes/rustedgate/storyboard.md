# rustedgate — storyboard

Duration 18 s, one continuous golden-hour shot.

1. **0–2.5 s — still gate.** The gate hangs ajar on rusty hinges; ruts lead
   through; the hawthorn holds its shape; dust motes drift in the gap.
   Caption: "the gate rests on its hinge".
2. **2.5–5 s — breeze swing.** The breeze worries the gate: it swings, checks
   on a low tuft, holds. Long shadow drags across the ruts. A robin lands on
   the latch. Caption: "a robin holds the latch".
3. **5–9 s — robin visit + second swing.** Tail bob, two hops along the top
   bar; the tuft lets go and the gate swings a little wider. Caption:
   "the field keeps working" (tractor crossing far off).
4. **9.5–12.5 s — pheasant burst.** The robin lifts away; a pheasant bursts
   from the grass at frame left; tractor glint. Caption: "wings over the wheat".
5. **13–18 s — crows, dog, settle.** Two crows drop onto the gate top and
   lift; a dog runs down the track; the gate settles ajar. Caption:
   "the evening lets it be".

Stills embedded at t = 1.5, 6.5, 10.4, 14.0, 17.0.
