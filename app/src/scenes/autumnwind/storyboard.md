# autumnwind — storyboard

24 s · 16:9 · 60 fps · three 8-second acts, continuous CameraRig drift
(gentle push toward the vortex site over the gravel).

| Act | Window | Beat | Camera | Captions |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–8 | windless gold | slow settle-in, high 3/4 on garden | FIG.01 STILL AIR |
| 2 | 8–16 | gust front + vortex | drift toward gravel, slight drop | FIG.02 THE GUST |
| 3 | 16–24 | settling quiet | hold on vortex site, leaves land | FIG.03 SETTLED |

## Shot logic

- **t≈2–4:** canopy reads, god rays visible, first leaves descending.
- **t≈9–13:** gust envelope swells — leaf speeds/lift rise, rays brighten,
  helical paths tighten.
- **t≈12.5–16.5:** ~24 leaves blend into a shrinking orbit over the gravel.
- **t≈13–15:** koi shadow crosses the pond corner once.
- **t≈17–21:** birds glide across the calm sky.
- **t≈18–23:** drifting leaves touch down on the rake lines and freeze —
  final frame shows them resting at seeded tilts.

## Focal points

1. Torii silhouette right third — the establishing anchor every act returns to.
2. Vortex site low center-right — camera drifts toward it through act 2.
3. Settled leaves on rake lines — the closing texture.
