| t | shot | beat | focal entry → exit |
|---|------|------|--------------------|
| 0–4 | low wide on barn front | still doorway: plank texture, swung door, interior hints, floor pool | eye lands on the door opening and drops to the floor pool |
| 4–8 | same | mote stream: dust thickens in the shaft, drifts down-left, shaft sways faintly | attention rides the mote drift upward toward the jamb rim |

| 16–20 | same | settle: motes thin, shaft steadies, cat holds | eye returns to the floor pool; rope sway is the last motion |

subtle). Lens ~40mm-equivalent (PerspectiveCamera fov 40, z 30); focal plane on
