# harborlight — storyboard (20s)

| t | shot | beat | focal entry → exit |
|---|------|------|--------------------|
| 0–5 | low wide orbit | cold open: tower emerges from dusk, beam swinging wide, gulls wheeling | eye rides the beam arc to the lamp |
| 5–10 | same, drifting closer | beam sweeps the water toward the camera — specular streak crosses frame | the streak leads the eye to the lens |
| 10–15 | close orbit | tower detail: whitewash, bands, rail, gulls bank against the lamp glow | rock skirt and door read on the waterline |
| 15–20 | wide pull | settle: beam keeps turning, gull orbits tighten, horizon swallows the light | the lamp is the last bright thing |

Camera: `CameraRig`-style low slow orbit — yaw drifts ~0.06 rad/s, radius
36→31, height ~2.5–4 m above the water; target eases from mid-tower toward
the lantern across the run. Beam yaw 2π/8s — it crosses the camera azimuth
around t≈7.5 and t≈15.5, laying the specular streak across the swell.
