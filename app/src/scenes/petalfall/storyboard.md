# petalfall — storyboard

Single continuous shot, 20s. CameraRig drifts slowly toward the stone lantern and tilts up into the falling column during the crescendo, then eases back down for the settle.

| t | Beat | Frame |
|---|------|-------|
| 0–2 | Establish: dusk garden, still canopy edge, moon-haze top-left, lantern lit, first lone petals | Wide, slight lateral drift; gravel bands + pond readable in the lower frame |
| 2–5 | Garden holds its breath — caption; koi shadow begins crossing the pond | Same wide hold; warm sky still dominant |
| 6–9 | Gust front arrives — sway amplitude + drift lift; wagtail enters and hops the gravel edge (pause-hop-pause) | Rig starts its drift toward the lantern; tilt begins |
| 9–13 | Petal-rain crescendo — dense falling column; petals catch on the lantern cap and rock; seed-puffs cross; “a gust loosens the canopy” | Tilted up into the falling column |
| 13–16 | Crescendo thins — caught petals dislodge, first petals settle flat on the gravel | Camera eases back down toward the garden floor |
| 16–20 | Quiet settling — petals resting on gravel and the pond corner as koi pass; sky fully cooled to dusk; “petals settle where the koi pass” | Low wide hold on the floor; fog drifts |

Sound cue targets: gust swell ~t 8, lantern glint swell ~t 12–16, fade to silence on the settle.
