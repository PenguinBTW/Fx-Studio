# storyboard — tidepools (20s)

| beat | window | action | caption |
|---|---|---|---|
| STILL MIRROR | 0–5.0s | Top-down. Wet rock ring frames the pool; sky reflection glass-flat; cloud shadows drift; starfish breathe, anemones sway gently; faint foam at the rim. | 01 THE POOL |
| FIRST RIPPLES | 5.0–9.0s | Droplet rings expand from seeded points; surf wavelets cross the pool as travelling stripes; caustics shimmer through the disturbance. | 02 RIPPLES |
| THE SLOSH | 9.0–12.5s | Camera tilts to the surf: pool compresses down, sea+sky band sweeps the top. A wavelet sloshes the rim — surge band crosses the pool, foam streaks, everything re-ripples. Hermit crab darts mid-chaos. | 03 THE SEA ARRIVES |
| GLASS AGAIN | 12.5–20s | Tilt settles back top-down; ripple energy decays; caustics sharpen; the mirror returns — hazier, warmer. Anemones calm, starfish keep breathing. | 04 GLASS AGAIN |
