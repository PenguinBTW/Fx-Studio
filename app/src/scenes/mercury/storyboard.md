# mercury — storyboard

1. **0–8s — first contact**: black void; a droplet swells into frame top, stretches,
   detaches, falls; impact — crownless absorption, pool surface rings and rebounds.
2. **8–18s — accumulation**: orbit begins; each absorption visibly swells the pool;
   specular glints sweep the surface as the camera moves.
3. **18–26s — settling**: cadence slows; wobbles decay; the surface tension wins.
4. **26–30s — mirror**: near-perfect sphere reflecting the key light, slow hero drift.
