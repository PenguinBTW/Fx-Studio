# meteorrain — storyboard

| t (s) | beat | frame |
|------:|------|-------|
| 0–3   | quiet sky — Milky Way band, airglow, dome slit glint, beacon blink | establishing wide |
| 3–10  | first meteors — sporadic streaks from the shared radiant, trails fading | same wide, sky dominant |
| 10–13 | fireball — headline bolide crosses, fragments into 3-4 colored secondaries | camera tilts skyward |
| 13–20 | lingering glow — smoke trails drift, occasional straggler meteors, fade | slow drift, sky relaxes |

Captions: "the sky keeps score" (t≈1.5) / "one of them fell apart" (t≈10) /
"and the smoke stays a while" (t≈15).
