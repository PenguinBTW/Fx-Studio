# frost — visual plan

Register: thermal-crystalline — the phase transition itself as subject. geode grows crystal in rock-time; frost grows it in a night, on glass, in feathers.

Subject: a windowpane macro at dawn. Condensation beads the night glass; dendritic ice feathers inward from the edges as temperature drops; the rising sun backlights the crystals — water becoming mineral in time-lapse.

Concept anchors:
- growth as narrative: the frost front IS the plot — edges first, dendrite veins leading, the rim catching light as it crystallizes
- light transformation: the same pane is dead blue glass at night and glowing amber mineral at dawn
- scale ambiguity: a windowpane shot like a frozen continent seen from orbit

Palette: night blue-black warming to amber/rose dawn; frost silver-blue igniting to gold.
Lighting: pure backlight — the sun never enters the frame, only its transmission through ice.
Composition: full-frame pane, edge-nucleated growth creating natural vignette structure.
Motion: growth front + dawn rise + camera breath only — no cuts.



## mapping table
| abstract concept | physical visual |
|---|---|
| time compression | growth front visibly creeping — phase change made spatial |
| cold | blue-steel palette, crystalline texture, stillness |
| warmth returning | amber transmission through ice — light physically filtered by the mineral |
| fragility/transience | feather edges, the melting-point thinness of the rim highlight |

## anti-cliché audit
- NOT a generic winter landscape with snowflakes — no snowfall cliché
- NOT a macro stock-photo frost — the growth must MOVE, the transition is the subject
- no lens flare, no bokeh balls, no cozy interior reveal

## focal hierarchy
1. the crystallizing rim (hero — always the sharpest, brightest element)
2. dendrite vein structure inside frozen regions
3. dawn color transformation of the whole pane
4. condensation beads (opening act only), sparkle glints (seasoning)
