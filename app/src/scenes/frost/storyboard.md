# frost — storyboard (30s continuous)

- 0–4s: night glass, condensation beads, deep blue stillness
- 4–12s: frost nucleates at edges and corners — dendrite feathers creep inward
- 8–26s: dawn rises behind the pane — blue thawing to amber, crystals catching fire
- 12–24s: growth front crosses mid-pane, veins leading, rim-highlight advancing
- 24–30s: pane half-feathered in glowing ice, twinkling glints, camera settling

## camera specification
- lens: 35mm-equivalent (PerspectiveCamera fov 35), macro intimacy
- camera vector: near-static macro, z 3.4→2.6 slow push, ±0.25 lateral drift
- focal points: pane center throughout; DoF aperture 4 on the glass surface
