# accretion — visual plan

Scale the catalog lacks: celestial. Gravity as choreographer — Keplerian shear is the only motion, and every grain's orbit is closed-form honest orbital mechanics (ω ∝ r^-1.5). The subject is time itself at cosmic scale: worlds assembling.

Don't forget to ground yourself when its time USE THE SKILL and REREAD IT ALL

## mapping table
| abstract concept | physical visual |
|---|---|
| gravity / orbital mechanics | Keplerian shear — inner bands visibly lapping outer |
| accretion / formation | the clump brightening as it gathers, gap opening in its lane |
| deep time | the patience of the camera; nothing hurries |
| radiant energy | the white-hot core, grains glinting as they catch it |

## anti-cliché audit
- NOT a NASA render rainbow — no false-color nebula gradients; the palette is starlight-white, dust-amber, void-black
- no lens flare sprite, no starfield sprinkle backdrop — the disk IS the scene
- no spiral-galaxy shape language — this is a flat proto-planetary disk, not a galaxy

## focal hierarchy
1. the core blaze — small, fierce, the light source
2. the streaming dust bands — shear made visible
3. the clump and its gap — the narrative event
4. glinting grains — seasoning

## light
Single point light at the core — physically the star itself; grains and clump lit by it alone. No fill.
