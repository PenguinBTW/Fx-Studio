# orrery — storyboard (20 s, 16:9 frame, f.p = progress)

Camera throughout: slow low orbit, 58 mm. Every frame: deep-black desk world,
one warm key high right, cold rim back-left, ember sun glowing.

## S1 — Establishing (0–4 s)
Wide-ish on the plinth. Ember sun flickers to life above its cup; gear train
already counters slowly on the column. Title "THE ORRERY" fades in
bottom-centre (1.1–4.6 s). Dust drifts through the key shaft.

## S2 — Clockwork (4–11 s)
The three rings and their brass arms turn at planet rates; planet_1 (rust)
crosses the frame-right third, planet_2 (sea marble) swings into the foreground
third — it owns the frame edge for a moment, soft-blurred by the radial DoF.
Caption "brass, stone, and a borrowed sun" holds 7.2–11.4 s.

## S3 — Push (11–20 s)
Camera pitch rises slightly, radius eases 4.75 → 3.7 toward the sun. Gear
shadows sweep the pedestal. Caption "every gear keeps its own time" 13.4–17.6 s.
Ember bloom swells gently into the final hold at 20 s.
