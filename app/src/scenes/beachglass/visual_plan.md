# beachglass — visual plan
Golden-hour macro shot lying almost on the sand: scattered sea-glass shards on a
wet mirror-plane beach, a low sun sweeping a glint lane across them.

## World
- Foreground: wet sand mirror-plane — sky reflection peach→teal, fine ripple grain.
- Mid: 9 seeded glass shards (seafoam/amber/cobalt/white/green) half-bedded, one
  large frosted bottle-glass hero at authored ~(0.62, 0.42).
- Far: blurred dune + grass silhouettes along the top edge (~y 0.82+).
- Sky implied only through the sand reflection + glint lane.

## Light
- Low sun right-to-left: a long specular lane sweeps across the sand (t 7→12).
- Each shard glows (transmission boost + caustic speckle under it) when the lane
  crosses its x; the hero flares hard once (~t 9.2).
- Warm gold key from the lane side; cool teal fill in the reflection.

## Life
- Thin surf line: white lace edge advances up to y~0.38 (t 4→6.5), holds, ebbs
  (t 10→13). Lacy foam halos wrap the shard bases while the edge passes.
- 3 sand crabs dart between shards on piecewise pause→dart→pause paths.
- Sandpiper silhouette works the waterline (t 5→18), peck-bobbing.
- Gull shadow crosses once (t 13.5→15).
- A small shell is deposited by the last wash (~t 11).

## Beats
0-4 still shards · 4-8 surf advance · 8-12 glint sweep · 12-15 crab skitters ·
15-20 ebb + settle.
