# beachglass — storyboard (20s, closed-form)
- t0-4   STILL SHARDS — mirror-sand, shards dark, faint breeze shimmer.
- t4-8   THE SAND BREATHES — surf lace edge creeps up-frame over the shards.
- t8-12  GLINT LANE — specular band sweeps left; shards ignite one by one;
         hero flare ~9.2; piper pecks the waterline.
- t12-15 SKITTERS — crabs dart shard-to-shard; gull shadow crosses; shell lands.
- t15-20 EBB — lace retreats, shards dim to silhouettes, gold deepens, settle.

Camera: very low macro (few cm off the sand), slow drift tracking the glint
lane leftward, gentle settle after the ebb. Probes: "hero-glass" on the bottle
piece, "surf-line" on the lace edge.
