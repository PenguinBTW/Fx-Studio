# mobile — visual plan

## Register
Kinetic sculpture — a Calder-style hanging mobile in a dark gallery void. Nothing in the catalog is suspended, balanced, or sculpture-as-subject: existing scenes are environments or phenomena; this is an *object in motion*, where the motion IS the artwork.

## Metaphor
Equilibrium as choreography. Each arm floats in slow rotation, paddles catching the light as they turn — the sculpture composes itself differently every second, never repeating, never settling. Time is the slow breathing of balance.

## Materials
- Arms: blackened steel wire rods, matte, thin — almost invisible against the void, so paddles appear to float
- Paddles: painted sheet metal — one red, one yellow, one blue, one black, one white (Calder primaries), subtle clearcoat sheen, irregular organic blob shapes
- Light: single museum spot from above-left; everything else falls to black

## Anti-cliché audit
- NOT a physics toy-demo look: no visible strings cluttering, no cartoon colors — restrained palette, gallery darkness
- NOT symmetric/tabby: arm lengths and paddle masses deliberately asymmetric; rotation rates incommensurate (no visible periodicity within 30s)
- NOT fast: real mobiles move at seconds-per-revolution — slow is the point

## Focal hierarchy
1. The red paddle (largest mass, lowest tier) — eye lands and returns
2. Rotation silhouettes of the whole composition
3. Negative space — the void is half the composition

## Changes over time
| Time | Beat | Change |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6s | reveal | camera drifts back from the red paddle; mobile emerges from darkness piece by piece |
| 6–22s | composition | slow orbit; arms rotate at incommensurate rates — the sculpture continuously recomposes; paddles flare as they turn through the light |
| 22–30s | hero settle | camera eases to stillness on a three-quarter view; rotation continues — a held gallery moment |
