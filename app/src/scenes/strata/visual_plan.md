# strata — visual plan

Geological-deep-time register: erosion as revelation. Sedimentary strata are deep time made visible — each band a milieu, their differential erosion the honest physics of how canyons read. The catalog has henge (human timekeeping) but nothing geological; strata is time written in rock itself.

Don't forget to ground yourself when its time USE THE SKILL and REREAD IT ALL

## mapping table
| metaphor | materials | changes over time |
|---|---|---|
| deep time | sedimentary bands — ochre/rust/bone strata with hard caprock | bands shear, retreat, and carve as compressed time passes |
| differential resistance | hard layers protrude, soft layers alcove | relief amplifies — the wall gains character |
| gravity's patience | scree fans at the wall base | fans grow as gullies incise |
| reading the past | exposed bedding planes | more layer interior exposed as erosion proceeds |

## anti-cliché audit
- NOT a grand-canyon postcard — no river, no sunset godbeams, single wall face studied closely
- no saturated red-rock tourism palette — bone/ochre/rust in honest desert varnish tones
- no vegetation green — this is bare geology

## focal hierarchy
1. the strata bands and their evolving relief
2. gully incision and scree growth — the motion
3. raking light across the carved face
4. sky sliver at top — scale reference only
