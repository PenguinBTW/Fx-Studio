# strata — storyboard

A canyon wall in compressed geological time. Sedimentary bands — ochre, rust, bone — shear and slump as erosion carves them: gullies deepen, scree fans grow at the base, hard caprock holds while soft layers retreat. The camera drifts along the wall as a million years pass in thirty seconds. 30s, single continuous shot.

## Beats
- 0–5s: the wall stands young — layered bands intact, sharp bedding planes.
- 5–15s: erosion begins — gullies incise the soft layers, the wall face roughens, scree fans grow.
- 15–25s: deep time — hard caprock now protrudes, soft bands retreat into alcoves, the wall reads as carved.
- 25–30s: hold — the eroded face in warm low light, every layer telling its resistance.

## Camera specification
- Lens: normal; aperture engine-scale — deep, no DoF needed at this scale.
- Position vector: slow lateral drift parallel to the wall, slight elevation rise — geological survey gaze.
- Focal point: the wall face at mid-height.
- Movement language: patient drift, geological time.
