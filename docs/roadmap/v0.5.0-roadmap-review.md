## 1. Executive Summary & Production Status

**f(x) Studio** has evolved from an experimental prototype into a production-grade procedural motion graphics and animation platform capable of rendering:
- **Commercials & 3D Product Teasers** (precision unibody reveals, exploded assemblies)
- **UI/UX Interaction Showcases** (spatial glass workspaces, card transitions, responsive typography)
- **Data Visualizations & Technical Infographics** (oscilloscopes, HUD telemetry, single-stroke vector CAD drafting)
- **Cinematic Procedural Music Videos** (procedural vehicles, synchronized lyrics, continuous multi-act transitions)

All four architectural modernization milestones have been completed and verified against production benchmarks.

## 3. Core Architectural Invariants Preserved

1. **Closed-Form Determinism**: Every primitive, particle, line, and transform evaluates as a pure function of timeline time:
   $$\text{Frame} = f(t)$$
   Guarantees bit-identical output whether scrubbed interactively or encoded at 4K in headless Chrome.
2. **WebGL State Cache Invariant**: `renderer.resetState()` enforces complete state cleanup after all custom GL passes, eliminating cross-scene texture or blend mode leakage.
3. **HalfFloat Linear HDR Compositor**: Recycled HalfFloat linear HDR targets (`makeRT`) with optical bloom pyramids, halation, chromatic aberration, and film grain.
4. **Sub-Frame Adaptive Supersampling (1–324 spp)**: True physical motion blur and temporal antialiasing without ghosting.
5. **Dynamic Audio & Timeline Decoupling**: Optional audio/speech synchronization via `ClockDriver`, preventing boot crashes when audio tracks are absent.

| **Automated Kinematic Telemetry** | 60fps optical flow, Sobel edge density, and CIE Lab $\Delta E_{76}$ palette discipline gating. | Three-Tier Review Hierarchy; zero blocking false-positives on benchmark suites. |
