## 1. Executive Summary

Version `v0.7.0` is a landmark engine overhaul that elevates **f(x) Studio** from a procedural motion graphics tool into a physically-grounded cinematic simulation and rendering engine. Built to fulfill all 14 prerequisites and 7 modernization milestones for the scientific narrative film ***First Light***, this release introduces full-spectrum physical optics, 500k-capacity GPU instanced particles, twist-free Bishop frame ribbon parallel transport, multi-tier volumetric raymarching, streamline untangling, in-world 3D PBR typography with thin-lens Circle-of-Confusion integration, and an EBU R128 broadcast-compliant procedural audio mastering pipeline.

Every new module is closed-form, deterministic ($f(t)$), leak-free, and passes 100% of automated unit, regression, and hardware-accelerated rendering suites.

## 2. Core Architectural Deliverables

### A. Physical Colour Science & Refraction Optics (P7a, P8, P11)
- **CIE 1931 & Planckian Blackbody (`spectrum.ts`, `glsl/spectrum.ts`)**:
  - Wyman-Sloan polynomial approximation for CIE 1931 XYZ colour matching functions ($360\text{--}830\,\text{nm}$).
  - Smooth-max gamut mapping (`softDesaturateToGamut`, $k=0.12$) eliminating rainbow strip seam kinks near zero-crossings.
  - Saturated $380\,\text{nm}$ violet locus $(0.28, 0, 1)$ without dark clipping.
  - Numerical integration of Planck's radiation law ($1000\text{--}40000\,\text{K}$) with luminance-normalized chromaticity tables.
- **Cauchy Dispersion & Dielectric Fresnel (`refract.ts`, `glsl/refract.ts`)**:
  - Physical Cauchy dispersion $n(\lambda) = A + B/\lambda^2$ calibrated for BK7 glass, water, diamond, and SF11 dense flint.
  - Exact unpolarized dielectric Fresnel equations and total internal reflection (TIR) energy conservation ($R + T = 1.0$).
  - Descartes spherical droplet caustics recovering primary ($42.37^\circ$) and secondary ($50.41^\circ$) rainbow angles with Alexander's dark band.
- **Physical Depth-of-Field & Thin-Lens Bokeh (`dof.ts`, `glsl/dof.ts`)**:
  - Thin-lens Circle-of-Confusion (CoC) solver: $\text{CoC} = |S_1 - f_d|/S_1 \cdot f_o^2 / (N(f_d - f_o))$.
  - Bilateral occlusion gather preventing background bokeh from bleeding over sharp foreground silhouettes.
  - Multi-tier sample counts (16/32/64 taps) with sub-pixel Poisson disk sampling.

### B. High-Capacity Particles & Vector Volumetrics (P4, P5, P6)
- **Large GPU Particles (`particles_large.ts`)**:
  - Instanced rendering handling up to 500,000 particles at 60 fps.
  - Closed-form history evaluation $t - k\Delta t$ for trail rendering without Euler integration drift.
  - Native field modes: `press`, `spiral`, `fall`, `star`, and `free` driven by 3D curl noise.
- **Bishop Frame Parallel Transport & Auroras (`ribbon.ts`, `aurora.ts`)**:
  - Wang et al. double-reflection Rotation-Minimizing Frame (RMF) algorithm eliminating Frenet 180° twist flip singularities across inflection points.
  - In-place GPU buffer updates (`updateFromSpine`) with zero per-frame heap allocations.
  - Atmospheric aurora curtains modeling quantum collision emission: $391.4\,\text{nm}$ ($N_2^+$ violet), $557.7\,\text{nm}$ (forbidden $O$ green), and $630.0\,\text{nm}$ (forbidden $O$ crimson).
- **Volumetric Raymarcher (`raymarch.ts`, `glsl/raymarch.ts`)**:
  - Composable GLSL raymarching framework with sphere-tracing over-relaxation and empty-space skipping.
  - Three standardized quality tiers (Tier 0 preview, Tier 1 preview+, Tier 2 offline) beating 33 ms/frame budget at 1080p.
  - Depth-aware bilateral upsampling pass for half-resolution volume evaluation.

### C. Procedural Geometry & Streamline Untangling (P12a, P12b)
- **Streamline Untangling (`treewalk.ts`)**:
  - `walkToStreamlines`: Transforms chaotic Brownian motion into smooth laminar magnetic field lines with $>60\%$ path length reduction and $>90\%$ curvature reduction.
  - Exact $C^0$ endpoint preservation across all morph parameters $s \in [0, 1]$.
- **Branching Tree Extraction (`mapWalkToTree`)**:
  - Extracts acyclic hierarchical trees from 3D polylines with guaranteed junction continuity.
- **Curve Morphing (`curvemorph.ts`)**:
  - Continuous vertex correspondence, automatic closed-loop rotation/reversal recovery, and Catmull-Rom polyline tweening.

### D. Kinetic Typography & In-World Scene Lighting (P10a, P10b)
- **Kinetic Counters & Glyphs (`textfx.ts`, `vectorglyphs.ts`, `hersheyglyphs.ts`)**:
  - Rolling mechanical odometer with continuous multi-digit carries ($99,999 \to 100,000$) and uniform tabular cell spacing.
  - Note-synced stroke reveals with exponential pen glow decays ($e^{-t/\tau}$).
  - Full 80-character stroked Hershey glyph library decoupled from rendering passes with quantised geometry caching.
- **In-World 3D Text Lighting (`text_lighting.ts`)**:
  - PBR scene lighting (`MeshStandardMaterial` / `MeshPhysicalMaterial`) with depth buffer participation (`depthWrite: true`, `depthTest: true`).
  - Integration into thin-lens DoF blur.
  - Volume-preserving radial deflection map $r' = (r^3 + R^3)^{1/3}$ repelling particles around letterforms without streamline crossings.

### E. Multi-Track Audio Mixdown & Broadcast Mastering (P13, P14)
- **Mastering & Loudness (`mixdown.ts`, `synth.ts`, `verify_audio.py`)**:
  - EBU R128 mastering to $-14.80\,\text{LUFS}$ integrated (target $-14 \pm 1$) and $-1.50\,\text{dBTP}$ true peak ($\le -1.0\,\text{dBTP}$).
  - Double-gated digital silence (`min|x| = 0.0, max|x| = 0.0`) eliminating ffmpeg loudnorm dither ringing during void intervals.
  - Lookahead sidechain ducking attenuating music stems prior to transient hits.
- **Procedural SFX Synthesizer (`synth.ts`)**:
  - Deterministic 48 kHz / 24-bit stereo cue synthesis with PolyBLEP anti-aliased oscillators and ZDF state variable filters.

### F. Rig Solvers & Automated Seam Continuity (P1a, P9)
- **Analytical Solvers (`rig.ts`)**:
  - Closed-form dolly-zoom focal length / distance solver: constant screen-projected height with relative error $< 4.44 \times 10^{-16}$.
  - Screen-space autofocus projection matching scene depth with relative error $< 4.86 \times 10^{-14}$.
  - Divergence-free curl noise with analytical Jacobian ($\text{Tr}(\mathbf{J}) \equiv 0$).
- **Automated Seam Harness (`check_seams.ts`)**:

## 4. Architectural Invariants Enforced

1. **Closed-Form Determinism ($f(t)$)**:
   All motion, particle positions, trails, camera paths, noise fields, and procedural audio cues evaluate purely as functions of timeline timestamp $t$. Forward, reverse, and arbitrary random timestamp scrubbing produce bitwise byte-identical output buffers.
2. **Zero-Allocation Inner Render Loops**:
   All per-frame animation and simulation passes reuse pre-allocated typed arrays (`Float32Array`) and static GPU vertex buffers. No objects or closures are created in rendering hot paths.
3. **WebGL State Isolation**:
   Every non-Three.js full-screen pass and raw shader pass calls `renderer.resetState()` upon completion to prevent state poisoning.
4. **Energy & Boundary Conservation**:
   Dielectric Fresnel equations conserve energy ($R + T = 1.0$), streamline deflection maps preserve fluid volume, and Bishop parallel transport frames remain strictly orthonormal via Gram-Schmidt stabilization.
